# Clinical translation and remaining gaps

## What has been established in patients

**Safety is the field's strongest claim.** Across allogeneic, cord-blood, iPSC-derived, engineered and cell-line
products, investigators consistently report no GvHD and little or no CRS/ICANS — including the CD19 CAR-NK
studies (PMID 32023374; Marin 2024, PMID 38238616), iPSC-derived FT596 (PMID 39798981), 4-1BB-costimulated
CD19 CAR-NK (PMID 40251398) and intracranial HER2 CAR-NK (PMID 37148198). This is the principal differentiator
from CAR-T therapy and holds across 120+ CAR-NK trials catalogued in the 2025 clinical landscape review
(PMID 40149002).

**Efficacy is real where three conditions coincide** — haematological target, armoured/preactivated product,
adequate lymphodepletion:

| Product | Setting | Key point |
|---|---|---|
| Haploidentical PB-NK + IL-2 | AML, non-transplant | first demonstration of in vivo expansion and remissions (PMID 15632206); Treg depletion raised expansion 10%→27% and improved clearance (PMID 24719405) |
| KIR-ligand-mismatched NK | elderly high-risk AML | feasible, no GvHD, CR in active disease (PMID 21791425) |
| CB-derived CAR19/IL-15 NK | r/r CD19+ B-cell malignancies | 11-patient phase 1/2 (PMID 32023374) then 37 patients with determinants of response (PMID 38238616) |
| CD19-BBz CAR-NK (BaEV-transduced CB) | r/r large B-cell lymphoma | repeat dosing feasible, phase 1 (PMID 40251398) |
| FT596 (iPSC; CD19 CAR + hnCD16 + IL-15/IL-15Rα) | B-cell lymphoma | first-in-human iPSC-derived CAR-NK (PMID 39798981) |
| Donor CIML NK | post-transplant relapse | expansion, persistence and efficacy characterised (PMID 35349491); original AML trial PMID 27655849 |
| CIML NK + N-803 ± ipilimumab | r/r head and neck cancer | first-in-human in solid tumour after median 6 prior lines (PMID 39948608) |
| AFM13-precomplexed CB-NK | CD30+ lymphoma refractory to BV **and** checkpoint inhibitors | phase 1 with meaningful responses in a dismal-prognosis population (PMID 40186077) |
| NK-92 / CAR-NK-92 | RCC, melanoma, advanced cancers, recurrent GBM | safe at high doses; irradiated so non-persistent (PMID 18836917, PMID 24094496, PMID 37148198) |
| TRACK NK (CB, sIL-15, PD-L1+) | NSCLC refractory to checkpoint inhibitors | interim engineered-NK trial report (PMID 39903538) |
| Autologous SNK01 + chemo/cetuximab | NSCLC post-TKI | phase I/IIa with preclinical companion (PMID 38538093) |

**Solid tumours remain largely unsolved.** The exceptions are instructive: intracranial delivery bypasses
trafficking (PMID 37148198), and the head-and-neck CIML trial combines state programming, cytokine support and
checkpoint blockade (PMID 39948608). Both add something beyond "infuse more NK cells".

## Contributions to efficacy, ranked by strength of clinical evidence

1. **IL-15 armouring or IL-15-containing engagers** — present in essentially every product with a clear response
   signal.
2. **Lymphodepletion plus host Treg control** — the oldest and best-quantified lever (PMID 24719405).
3. **Memory-like (IL-12/15/18) programming** — clinical expansion/persistence data in two settings.
4. **Donor/education selection (KIR B haplotype, HLA-B −21, KIR-ligand mismatch)** — outcome associations in
   transplant cohorts (PMID 20581313, PMID 30647027).
5. **Non-cleavable/high-affinity CD16 plus an antibody or engager** — mechanistically strong, now in
   clinical products (FT596, AFM13-NK).
6. **Brake knockouts (CISH, NKG2A, TGF-β pathway)** — compelling preclinical data, clinical readouts still
   immature.
7. **Route of administration for solid tumours** — one positive trial (intracavitary GBM).

## Gaps this literature makes visible

- **No standard potency assay.** Cryopreservation-induced loss of 3-D function is invisible to conventional
  release assays (PMID 33067467), so "the product met specification" does not imply it was potent.
- **Persistence remains weeks at best.** Cell-intrinsic IL-15 helps; nothing yet produces the multi-month
  persistence CAR-T achieves, and repeat dosing (PMID 40251398) is the pragmatic answer.
- **Manufacturing platforms have never been compared head-to-head** on shared donors with a shared potency
  readout: feeder versus PM21 versus feeder-free; flask versus G-Rex versus bioreactor; DMSO versus DMSO-free.
- **Multi-edit products outpace their safety science.** Screens are generating edit lists faster than
  translocation/clonality assessment frameworks mature (PMID 41425600).
- **Solid-tumour trafficking and TME resistance are still the bottleneck** — hypoxia, TGF-β, lactylation,
  nutrient competition, CAF decoys and within-hours functional impairment on tumour entry (PMID 38267402).
- **Cost of goods and dose economics are essentially unreported**, despite being the main practical argument for
  allogeneic, iPSC and off-the-shelf approaches.
- **Reporting heterogeneity** — expansion fold, E:T ratios, targets and assay formats differ so widely that
  quantitative meta-analysis across the process literature is currently impossible.

## What a maximally-supported product would look like today

Cord-blood- or iPSC-derived NK cells; expanded with membrane-bound IL-21/4-1BBL feeders or PM21 particles in a
closed, gas-permeable or bioreactor system with serum-free medium; transduced (BaEV lentivirus) with an
NK-optimised CAR plus IL-15; edited for *CISH*, *KLRC1* and TGF-β resistance with hnCD16 retained; given a
terminal IL-12/15/18 memory-like preactivation; cryopreserved in a DMSO-minimised formulation and released
against a 3-D post-thaw potency assay; infused after lymphodepletion, with repeat dosing, an antibody or engager
partner, and intratumoral delivery where anatomy permits.

Every element of that sentence is supported by at least one study in this collection; **no published product
combines all of them**, which is a reasonable statement of where the field is.
