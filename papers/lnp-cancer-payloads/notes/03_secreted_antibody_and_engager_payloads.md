# 03 — Secreted antibody, nanobody and T-cell-engager payloads

A coherent 23-paper theme encodes **secreted binding proteins** — bispecific T-cell engagers (BiTEs), macrophage engagers, nanobody-BiTEs, and antibody/cytokine fusions — as mRNA/saRNA in LNPs, so the patient's tissue (usually liver, or the local tumour) becomes a continuous in situ "biofactory". The pitch versus recombinant-protein engagers is sustained exposure from periodic dosing, avoidance of protein-manufacturing/folding/aggregation problems, and the ability to localise expression.

## mRNA-encoded T-cell engagers (organ-restricted secretion)

- **MTS105 / GPC3 TCE (clinical-stage).** Huang 2025 (*Nat Commun*, PMID 41397962) encodes a Glypican-3 × CD3 T-cell engager delivered by LNP to the liver; higher liver-vs-plasma exposure, complete dose-dependent regression of liver-orthotopic HCC, favourable linear PK in mice/rats/cynomolgus, no severe AEs, and a first-in-human study underway. This is the template "in situ TCE" paper.
- **Dual bispecifics in one particle.** Che 2026 (PMID 41884275) co-encodes EpCAM×CD3 and Claudin18.2×4-1BB BsAbs in a single mRNA-LNP ("E3C4"), delivered intraperitoneally for gastric-cancer peritoneal metastasis, achieving dual-antigen T-cell co-activation locally while avoiding systemic toxicity.

## Nanobody-based engagers and saRNA

- Zhu 2026 (PMID 41882748) constructs the first CLDN6-nanobody BiTE encoded by **self-amplifying mRNA** and delivered by LNP for ovarian cancer; cross-species (human/mouse/NHP) CLDN6 reactivity, sustained in vivo expression, durable tumour control without systemic toxicity — nanobody format chosen to avoid scFv folding/aggregation/stability issues.

## Macrophage engagers and TME bridging

- Zhang 2026 (*Nat Commun*, PMID 41965349) delivers mRNA encoding a bispecific **macrophage** engager (BiME, ErbB2 on glioma × CD206 on M2 macrophages) via a pH-responsive charge-reversal LNP that also releases a surface-conjugated PD-L1 antibody on glutathione trigger — bridging macrophages to tumour cells, repolarising M2→M1, and coordinating innate + adaptive immunity in glioblastoma.

## Antibody/cytokine fusions and LNP surface antibodies

- Pan 2026 (PMID 40908442) encodes an IFN-α/anti-GPC3 fusion (see notes/02) — an antibody-targeted cytokine expressed in situ.
- Park 2025 (*ACS Nano*, PMID 39908463) uses an apolipoprotein fusion to spontaneously decorate mRNA-LNPs with a targeting antibody, blending payload (e.g. PTEN mRNA, see notes/04) with antibody-directed delivery.

## Design observations

1. **Liver as the deliberate factory.** Where the target antigen is hepatic (GPC3) or systemic exposure is acceptable, authors exploit, rather than fight, the LNP's hepatic tropism to get high, sustained engager titres (Huang 2025).
2. **Local routes for peritoneal/CNS disease.** Intraperitoneal (Che 2026) and brain-targeted (Zhang 2026) delivery localise engager expression and limit the on-target/off-tumour and cytokine-storm liabilities that constrain systemic BiTEs.
3. **saRNA to lower dose.** Self-amplifying backbones (Zhu 2026) are used to sustain engager output from less RNA.
4. **Format choice driven by manufacturability.** Nanobody/VHH formats are repeatedly preferred over scFv for in situ expression robustness.
