# 06 — Self-amplifying, replicon, small-activating, and circular RNA payloads

This note groups the **non-linear-mRNA coding scaffolds** used to get more, or longer, or less-immunogenic payload expression at lower dose: self-amplifying / self-replicating RNA (saRNA/srRNA) and alphavirus replicons; small-activating RNA (RNAa); and circular RNA (circRNA) / circular single-stranded DNA (cssDNA).

## Self-amplifying / replicon RNA (9 papers)

The recurring payload is, again, IL-12 — the amplification is precisely what lets a toxic cytokine be dosed safely:
- Li 2020 (*Nat Cancer*, PMID 34447945): srRNA-LNP ("LNP-replicon") combining an ICD-promoting lipid, danger-sensor-stimulating RNA, and IL-12; single intratumoural dose eradicates large tumours and gives abscopal regression.
- Wang 2024 (*Sci Rep*, PMID 38548896): "JCXH-211" srRNA-IL-12 dosed *intravenously* as well as intratumourally; safe in mice and NHP, inhibits PMN-MDSC infiltration, synergises with anti-PD-1.
- Li 2025 (*Commun Biol*, PMID 40851032): "CATP" cascade amplification — saRNA + modified mRNA encoding alphavirus capsid/envelope so transfected cells package and infect neighbours; 525-fold intratumoural IL-12, single-cycle spread for safety.
- Hu 2026 (PMID 41486691): "SAMVIG" integrates an alphavirus replicon + payload + VSV-G in one mRNA to generate enveloped virus-like vesicles in vivo, reproducing oncolytic-virus benefits without the manufacturing/biosafety/anti-vector-immunity constraints; modular for checkpoint-inhibitor payloads with no apparent length limit.
- Reznik 2024 (PMID 38919399): saRNA encoding the tumour suppressor NDRG2.

## Small-activating RNA (RNAa) — the prehistory

The oldest LNP "novel-payload" cancer papers use lipidoid/LNP-formulated double-stranded RNA to *induce endogenous* genes by targeting promoters:
- Place 2012 (*Mol Ther Nucleic Acids*, PMID 23343884): lipidoid-LNP saRNA inducing endogenous *p21*; intratumoural prostate-xenograft growth inhibition.
- Ren 2013 (*Prostate*, PMID 23836514): LNP saRNA inducing *NKX3-1*, upregulating p21/p27 and suppressing VEGFC.
- Kang 2012 (*JoVE*, PMID 22872227): intravesical saRNA in an orthotopic bladder-tumour model.

These prefigure the modern tumour-suppressor-restoration theme (notes/04) but achieve it by transcriptional activation rather than mRNA add-back.

## Circular RNA and circular ssDNA (9 papers)

circRNA is pursued for exceptional stability, prolonged protein output, and cost-effective production:
- Niu 2026 (PMID 41027739): in-vitro-synthesised circRNA encoding cytosine-deaminase–UPRT (circCDUPRT) + 5-fluorocytosine for gene-directed enzyme-prodrug therapy, combined with an IL-15 circRNA to expand CD8 T/NK cells (bridges notes/02, notes/07).
- Fu 2026 (PNAS, PMID 41843671): dual circRNAs encoding a SIGLEC9 chimeric switch receptor to engineer CAR-macrophages in glioblastoma (notes/01).
- Zhang 2025 (*Cancer Res*, PMID 39531509): intratumoural LNP-encapsulated circRNA cEMSY induces immunogenic cell death via mitochondrial TDP-43 aggregation → cGAS-STING activation, synergising with PD-1 blockade in lung adenocarcinoma.
- Hu 2026 (PMID 41896926): circular single-stranded DNA (cssDNA)-LNP for IL-12, far less immunogenic/toxic than plasmid DNA.
- Review: Lv 2026 (*Transl Oncol*, PMID 41353994) on circRNAs in cancer immunology and nanomedicine synergies.

## Common thread

All of these scaffolds exist to break the dose–toxicity–durability trade-off of linear mRNA: amplify (saRNA/replicon), persist (circRNA), induce-rather-than-supply (RNAa), or de-immunise the vector (cssDNA). IL-12 and other high-potency immunostimulants are the payloads that benefit most, which is why they recur here.
