## Core — ILDR2 antibodies, Fc biologics, and checkpoint biology

### ILDR2-Fc Is a Novel Regulator of Immune Homeostasis and Inducer of Antigen-Specific Immune Tolerance.
*Podojil JR, Hecht I, Chiang MY et al. — Journal of immunology (Baltimore, Md. : 1950), 2018. PMID 29431690 · PMC6830572 · [10.4049/jimmunol.1700326](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6830572/)*  
Topics: `Fc_fusion;immune_tolerance;autoimmunity;EAE;type1_diabetes;transplant`  

ILDR2-Fc as a tolerance drug: short-term dosing gives durable benefit in relapsing-remitting EAE, NOD type-1 diabetes, and minor-mismatch BMT engraftment; mechanism is **Treg induction / antigen-specific tolerance**, not T-cell deletion.

### ILDR2 Is a Novel B7-like Protein That Negatively Regulates T Cell Responses.
*Hecht I, Toporik A, Podojil JR et al. — Journal of immunology (Baltimore, Md. : 1950), 2018. PMID 29431694 · PMC6860365 · [10.4049/jimmunol.1700325](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6860365/)*  
Topics: `antibody_discovery;checkpoint_biology;Fc_fusion;T_cell_inhibition;autoimmunity;B7_family`  

Founding immune paper: identifies ILDR2 as a novel **B7-like** T-cell inhibitor; an ILDR2-ectodomain-Fc fusion binds a putative T-cell counter-receptor and is beneficial in collagen-induced arthritis and shuts down inflammatory cytokines in macrophage+T-cell cocultures. Frames ILDR2 for both autoimmunity and cancer.

### Characterization of BAY 1905254, an Immune Checkpoint Inhibitor Targeting the Immunoglobulin-Like Domain Containing Receptor 2 (ILDR2).
*Huetter J, Gritzan U, Gutcher I et al. — Cancer immunology research, 2020. PMID 32312711 · [10.1158/2326-6066.cir-19-0321](https://doi.org/10.1158/2326-6066.cir-19-0321)*  
Topics: `therapeutic_antibody;BAY_1905254;checkpoint_blockade;tumor_immunology;antagonist_mAb`  

**BAY 1905254** (Bayer): human/mouse/monkey cross-reactive anti-ILDR2 **hIgG2 antagonist**; ILDR2 found on lymph-node FRCs; antibody boosts T-cell activation and antigen-specific proliferation/cytotoxicity, is efficacious in syngeneic tumors (efficacy ∝ mutational load), and is additive/synergistic with anti-PD-L1, docetaxel, and tumor-antigen immunization.

### The Regulatory Cross-Talk between microRNAs and Novel Members of the B7 Family in Human Diseases: A Scoping Review.
*Ahangar NK, Hemmat N, Khalaj-Kondori M et al. — International journal of molecular sciences, 2021. PMID 33800752 · PMC7962059 · [10.3390/ijms22052652](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7962059/)*  
Topics: `review;checkpoint_biology;B7_family;microRNA;immunoregulation`  

Open-access PRISMA scoping review of microRNA cross-talk with newer B7 family checkpoints (B7-H3/H4/H5, BTNL2, B7-H6/H7, **ILDR2**); useful for situating ILDR2 among druggable B7 members and for miR-based regulation leads.

### The Positive and Negative Immunoregulatory Role of B7 Family: Promising Novel Targets in Gastric Cancer Treatment.
*Bolandi N, Derakhshani A, Hemmat N et al. — International journal of molecular sciences, 2021. PMID 34639059 · PMC8509619 · [10.3390/ijms221910719](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8509619/)*  
Topics: `review;checkpoint_biology;B7_family;tumor_immunology;gastric_cancer`  

Open-access review of the positive/negative immunoregulatory B7 family in gastric cancer; its **dedicated ILDR2 section** (domain architecture, expression, ILDR2-Fc, and a clear BAY 1905254 summary) is the best freely available secondary source for the paywalled BAY paper.

### Expression analysis of inhibitory B7 family members in Alzheimer's disease.
*Sabaie H, Tamimi P, Gharesouran J et al. — Metabolic brain disease, 2023. PMID 37665469 · [10.1007/s11011-023-01274-8](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11011-023-01274-8)*  
Topics: `checkpoint_biology;B7_family;expression_profiling;neuroinflammation`  

qPCR profiling of inhibitory B7 family members (incl. **ILDR2**) in peripheral blood in Alzheimer's disease — evidence that ILDR2 checkpoint expression is measurable and disease-modulated outside oncology.

### Sublingual macrophage-associated ILDR2 contributes to immune tolerance via Treg induction.
*Sultana F, Widyagarini A, Kawano Y et al. — Biochemical and biophysical research communications, 2025. PMID 39626366 · [10.1016/j.bbrc.2024.151009](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bbrc.2024.151009)*  
Topics: `research_antibody;monoclonal_antibody;immune_tolerance;Treg;macrophage;mucosal`  

Uses a **newly developed anti-mouse-ILDR2 monoclonal antibody** to show a CD206hi sublingual-mucosa macrophage subset displays surface ILDR2, is M2-skewed, and preferentially induces Foxp3+ Tregs — surface confirmation of ILDR2 on a tolerogenic myeloid population.


## Context — ILDR2 target biology

### Positional cloning of "Lisch-Like", a candidate modifier of susceptibility to type 2 diabetes in mice.
*Dokmanovic-Chouinard M, Chung WK, Chevre JC et al. — PLoS genetics, 2008. PMID 18654634 · PMC2464733 · [10.1371/journal.pgen.1000137](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2464733/)*  
Topics: `target_biology;positional_cloning;diabetes_modifier;beta_cell;Lisch_like`  

Positional cloning of **"Lisch-Like"** (Ildr2) as a distal-Chr1 type-2-diabetes modifier; links the gene to reduced β-cell replication/mass and mild hyperglycemia. The molecule's first functional identity.

### Analysis of the 'angulin' proteins LSR, ILDR1 and ILDR2--tricellulin recruitment, epithelial barrier function and implication in deafness pathogenesis.
*Higashi T, Tokuda S, Kitajiri S et al. — Journal of cell science, 2013. PMID 23239027 · [10.1242/jcs.116442](https://doi.org/10.1242/jcs.116442)*  
Topics: `target_biology;angulin;tight_junction;tricellulin`  

Defines the **angulin family** (LSR/ILDR1/ILDR2) at tricellular contacts and shows angulins recruit tricellulin to build the tricellular tight junction; ties barrier function to deafness. (J Cell Sci; not OA in this environment.)

### ILDR2: an endoplasmic reticulum resident molecule mediating hepatic lipid homeostasis.
*Watanabe K, Watson E, Cremona ML et al. — PloS one, 2013. PMID 23826244 · PMC3691114 · [10.1371/journal.pone.0067234](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3691114/)*  
Topics: `target_biology;hepatic_lipid;ER;metabolism;diabetes`  

ILDR2 as an **ER-resident** protein regulating **hepatic lipid homeostasis**; transient overexpression relieves steatosis in ob/ob mice.

### The genetic basis of obesity-associated type 2 diabetes (diabesity) in polygenic mouse models.
*Joost HG, Schürmann A — Mammalian genome : official journal of the International Mammalian Genome Society, 2014. PMID 24752583 · PMC4164836 · [10.1007/s00335-014-9514-2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4164836/)*  
Topics: `review;diabetes;obesity;genetics`  

Review of the genetic basis of obesity-associated T2D ("diabesity") in polygenic mouse models; situates Ildr2/Lisch-Like among modifier loci.

### [Molecular organization of tricellular tight junctions].
*Furuse M — Yakugaku zasshi : Journal of the Pharmaceutical Society of Japan, 2014. PMID 24790043 · [10.1248/yakushi.14-00006-1](https://doi.org/10.1248/yakushi.14-00006-1)*  
Topics: `review;tight_junction;angulin;tricellular_junction`  

Japanese-language review of the molecular organization of tricellular tight junctions (angulins/tricellulin context). (Yakugaku Zasshi; not OA.)

### Tricellular Tight Junctions in the Inner Ear.
*Kitajiri S, Katsuno T — BioMed research international, 2016. PMID 27195292 · PMC4852330 · [10.1155/2016/6137541](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4852330/)*  
Topics: `review;tight_junction;inner_ear;angulin`  

Review of tricellular tight junctions in the inner ear, placing angulins (incl. ILDR2/angulin-3) in barrier/deafness biology.

### ZNF70, a novel ILDR2-interacting protein, contributes to the regulation of HES1 gene expression.
*Watanabe K, Nakayama K, Ohta S et al. — Biochemical and biophysical research communications, 2016. PMID 27353377 · [10.1016/j.bbrc.2016.06.124](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bbrc.2016.06.124)*  
Topics: `target_biology;interactome;ZNF70;HES1;transcription`  

Identifies **ZNF70** as a novel ILDR2-interacting protein contributing to **HES1** regulation. (BBRC; not OA.)

### Angulin proteins ILDR1 and ILDR2 regulate alternative pre-mRNA splicing through binding to splicing factors TRA2A, TRA2B, or SRSF1.
*Liu Y, Nie H, Liu C et al. — Scientific reports, 2017. PMID 28785060 · PMC5547134 · [10.1038/s41598-017-07530-z](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5547134/)*  
Topics: `target_biology;angulin;pre_mRNA_splicing;ILDR1`  

Shows angulins ILDR1 (and family) bind splicing factors **TRA2A/TRA2B/SRSF1** and regulate alternative pre-mRNA splicing with nuclear translocation — a non-junctional angulin role potentially shared by ILDR2.

### Tricellular tight junction-associated angulins in the gill epithelium of rainbow trout.
*Kolosov D, Kelly SP — American journal of physiology. Regulatory, integrative and comparative physiology, 2018. PMID 29631364 · PMC6139612 · [10.1152/ajpregu.00431.2017](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6139612/)*  
Topics: `target_biology;angulin;tight_junction;comparative_physiology`  

Comparative physiology: angulin transcripts including **ildr2** track barrier development in rainbow-trout gill epithelium.

### ILDR2 has a negligible role in hepatic steatosis.
*Millings EJ, De Rosa MC, Fleet S et al. — PloS one, 2018. PMID 29847571 · PMC5976177 · [10.1371/journal.pone.0197548](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5976177/)*  
Topics: `target_biology;hepatic_steatosis;knockout;metabolism`  

**Negative control / walk-back:** Cre liver-specific Ildr2 knockout does NOT cause steatosis ('negligible role in hepatic steatosis'); cautions that earlier shRNA phenotypes were likely off-target.

### ILDR2 stabilization is regulated by its interaction with GRP78.
*Watanabe K, Nakayama K, Ohta S et al. — Scientific reports, 2021. PMID 33863978 · PMC8052334 · [10.1038/s41598-021-87884-7](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8052334/)*  
Topics: `target_biology;interactome;protein_stability;GRP78;beta_cell;ER`  

Identifies ER chaperones **GRP78 and PDIA1** as ILDR2 interactors and shows **GRP78 stabilizes ILDR2** by blocking ubiquitin–proteasome degradation (ER-stress-linked) — implies ILDR2 surface density is regulated, relevant to antibody target availability.

### Bicellular Localization of Tricellular Junctional Protein Angulin-3/ILDR2 Allows Detection of Podocyte Injury.
*Higashi AY, Saito AC, Higashi T et al. — The American journal of pathology, 2024. PMID 38311119 · [10.1016/j.ajpath.2024.01.008](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ajpath.2024.01.008)*  
Topics: `research_antibody;monoclonal_antibody;angulin3;tight_junction;podocyte;kidney`  

Uses a **new anti-angulin-3/ILDR2 monoclonal antibody** to map ILDR2 localization in podocytes and detect podocyte injury by its tricellular→bicellular redistribution.

### The role of the tricellular junction protein ILDR2 in glomerulopathies: Expression patterns and functional insights.
*Siegerist F, Kliewe F, Hammer E et al. — iScience, 2024. PMID 39640577 · PMC11617378 · [10.1016/j.isci.2024.111329](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11617378/)*  
Topics: `target_biology;angulin3;tight_junction;podocyte;kidney;CLDN5`  

Shows ILDR2 is strongly expressed in **podocytes**, co-localizes and complexes with **CLDN5**, and is altered in glomerulopathies — a kidney-barrier role with on-target/off-tumor relevance.

### Identification of amino acids essential for angulin-1/3 binding of the tricellular tight junction binder, angubindin-1.
*Kuzu T, Iwashita Y, Tachibana K et al. — FEBS open bio, 2026. PMID 40928054 · PMC12767772 · [10.1002/2211-5463.70113](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12767772/)*  
Topics: `structure;angulin;tight_junction;angubindin;binding_residues`  

Alanine-scanning identifies the **angubindin-1** residues required for binding angulin-1/-3 (ILDR2) — structural insight into the angulin/tTJ binding interface and tTJ-modulator engineering.
