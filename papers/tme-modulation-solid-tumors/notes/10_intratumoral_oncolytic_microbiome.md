# Local biological modulation: intratumoral therapy, oncolytic viruses, engineered bacteria, microbiome

## Intratumoral and oncolytic approaches

The rationale is the same localisation argument as for cytokines, executed with a
replicating agent. GM-CSF-armed oncolytic adenovirus established the template
(PMID 20484030); oncolytic virotherapy increases intratumoral T-cell infiltration and
improves anti-PD-1 activity (PMID 28886381), which is the mechanistic claim that
justifies the combination. Engineered bacteria are the parallel platform (Salmonella
secreting heterologous flagellin, PMID 28179508).

Glioblastoma is where the clinical evidence concentrates, because local delivery is
already the surgical norm: G207 in paediatric high-grade glioma (PMID 33838625), a
CEA-expressing measles derivative in recurrent GBM (phase 1, PMID 38216554), and a trial
linking oncolytic immunoactivation to survival (PMID 37853118). Neoadjuvant anti-PD-1 in
GBM shows intratumoral plus systemic immune responses with a survival signal
(PMID 30742122) — evidence that timing, not just route, is a modulation variable.
VG161 has activity in refractory HCC (PMID 40108464).

Mechanistically the most interesting recent result is that elimination of solid tumours
requires intratumoral immune *triads* — CD4/CD8/DC co-localisation (PMID 38906155) —
which explains why importing a single cell type or agonist is insufficient. The
counterweight is that the TME represses T-cell mitochondrial biogenesis, causing
intratumoral metabolic insufficiency (PMID 27496732): a locally injected agent still
delivers effectors into a hostile metabolic environment.

## Microbiome modulation

This is the corpus's clearest demonstration that acquired immunotherapy resistance can be
reversed in humans by modifying the host rather than the tumour. Preclinical anchor: the
efficacy of CTLA-4 blockade depends on gut microbiota (PMID 26541610). Two independent
FMT trials converted anti-PD-1-refractory melanoma patients to responders
(PMID 33542131, PMID 33303685); a subsequent phase 1 confirmed feasibility
(PMID 37414899) and a phase 2 (FMT-LUMINate) extends the approach to NSCLC and melanoma
(PMID 41606121). Gut bacterial composition also drives *primary* resistance in RCC
(PMID 32376136, indexed under `clinical_resistance`).

The intratumoral microbiome is a distinct problem: pancreatic tumour bacteria induce
innate and adaptive suppression (PMID 29567829) and tumour microbiome diversity tracks
PDAC outcomes (PMID 31398337) — with the therapeutic twist that killing intratumoral
bacteria with a liposomal antibiotic generates immunogenic neoantigens (PMID 37749267).

## Open question

Donor selection, engraftment durability and mechanism attribution for FMT remain
unresolved; there is no defined bacterial consortium in this corpus with clinical
equivalence to whole-stool transplant.
