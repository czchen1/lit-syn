# Innate sensing agonists and engineered cytokines

Two axes are treated together because they share a single limiting problem: both act on
potent, evolutionarily conserved inflammatory circuits whose therapeutic index is set by
*where* the drug acts, not by target validity.

## Innate sensing (STING, TLR, RIG-I, type-I IFN)

Mechanistically the corpus is unusually coherent: cytosolic DNA sensing through
cGAS-STING is what makes a tumour immunogenic in the first place (PMID 25517615,
PMID 27367184, PMID 31852718), micronuclei formed after DNA damage are the physiological
source of that signal (PMID 28759889), and therefore DNA-damage-response inhibition
promotes antitumour immunity through STING (SCLC, PMID 30777870). Radiotherapy remodels
the inflammatory microenvironment through the same route (PMID 32161398).

The clinical problem is delivery and durability: free cyclic dinucleotides are cleared
and poorly cell-penetrant, motivating endosomolytic carriers (PMID 30664751), non-CDN
STING mimics (PMID 38480836), and metal-ion adjuvants (Mn²⁺, PMID 32839553). DC-intrinsic
STING licensing of cDC1 is where the required activity actually sits (PMID 38363830).
Counter-evidence worth keeping: chronic STING activation by mtDNA from senescent tumour
cells *drives* PMN-MDSC suppression (PMID 40203808) — pathway direction depends on
duration and cell type, which is the likely reason intratumoral STING agonist trials
underdelivered relative to preclinical data.

## Engineered cytokines

IL-12 dominates the axis because it is the most potent and the most toxic. The corpus
tracks the entire localisation strategy: regulatable IL-12 gene therapy in recurrent
high-grade glioma (phase 1, PMID 31413142), immunocytokines for GBM (PMID 33028706),
IL-12-armoured CAR-T (PMID 33469002, PMID 37041154), and IL-12 reprogramming of CAR-NKT
cells (PMID 38167707). IL-15 superagonist plus nivolumab in PD-1-refractory NSCLC is the
main clinical test of a γc-cytokine as a resistance-reversal agent (ALT-803,
PMID 29628312).

The suppressive-cytokine side is equally represented: macrophage IL-10 blocks
chemotherapy-dependent CD8 responses by suppressing intratumoral DC IL-12
(PMID 25446896), IL-10 promotes liver metastasis via PD-L1 induction (PMID 38160941), and
macrophage IL-10 output is sustained by mitochondrial complex III (PMID 39841829).
Note the deliberate tension with `cell_therapy_tme`, where IL-10 *expressed by* CAR-T
cells prevents dysfunction (PMID 38168996): the same cytokine is suppressive in the
myeloid compartment and metabolically restorative in the T-cell compartment.

CANTOS provides the outlier result — IL-1β blockade reducing incident lung cancer in an
atherosclerosis population (PMID 28855077) — the only chemoprevention-scale evidence
that dampening tumour-promoting inflammation changes cancer incidence.

## Open question

For both axes: whether tumour-restricted activation (masked cytokines, prodrugs,
regulated vectors, intratumoral routes) can widen the therapeutic index enough for
systemic use, and whether pulsatile rather than continuous innate activation avoids the
MDSC-inducing chronic phenotype.
