# Myeloid compartment: TAMs, MDSCs, neutrophils, phagocytosis checkpoints

## Mechanism

The myeloid compartment is the numerically dominant immune population in most solid
tumours and the most consistently suppressive. The corpus supports three separable
functions: (i) recruitment-driven suppression, where CCL2/CSF1 pull monocytes into the
tumour and locally polarise them (PMID 21654748, PMID 24056773); (ii) cytokine-mediated
T-cell shutdown, most directly shown in glioblastoma where myeloid IL-10 drives
intratumoral T-cell dysfunction (PMID 35177622); and (iii) neutrophil plasticity, where
TGF-β sets an "N2" suppressive phenotype but early-stage human lung TANs can be
stimulatory (PMID 19732719, PMID 25384214) — the compartment is context-dependent, not
uniformly hostile.

## Human evidence

- CSF1R inhibition remains the cleanest preclinical proof that macrophage
  re-polarisation, not depletion, drives benefit (glioma, PMID 24056773); clinical
  translation as monotherapy has been weak.
- IL-8 blockade (HuMax-IL8/BMS-986253) completed phase 1 in metastatic solid tumours and
  is the most direct human test of the myeloid-recruitment axis (PMID 31488216).
- Prostate cancer provides the strongest recent human resistance story: longitudinal
  profiling shows myeloid-mediated resistance *evolving* under therapy rather than being
  fixed at baseline (PMID 39633050).
- CAR-macrophages have entered the clinic for HER2-overexpressing solid tumours
  (phase 1, PMID 39920391) — a shift from suppressing myeloid cells to using them as the
  effector.

## What failed and why

Single-node myeloid blockade (CSF1R, CCR2) is compensated: depleting one recruitment
axis promotes another, and TAM subsets are spatially and temporally heterogeneous
(PMID 39068459). The current framing in the reviews (PMID 37211559, PMID 39780248) is
that myeloid targeting is a combination partner, indexed to which subset dominates a
given tumour.

## Open question

Which myeloid state is the actionable one? Subset-level markers (TREM2, SPP1, MARCO)
are better defined than subset-level therapeutics, and no trial in this corpus selects
patients by myeloid state.
