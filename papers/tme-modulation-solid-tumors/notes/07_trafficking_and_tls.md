# T-cell trafficking, chemokines and tertiary lymphoid structures

## Mechanism

Infiltration is a chemokine problem with a stromal gatekeeper. The corpus supports the
CXCL9/CXCL10–CXCR3 axis as the effector-recruitment route and CXCL12–CXCR4 as the
exclusion route (PMID 16269611, PMID 38066335). The decisive mechanistic result is that
CAF-derived CXCL12 coats tumour cells and keeps T cells out of PDAC (PMID 24277834);
conversely, CXCL9/10-engineered DCs increase infiltration and checkpoint-blockade
efficacy in lung cancer (PMID 38518770).

Tertiary lymphoid structures (TLS) reframe the problem: rather than importing effectors,
generate them locally. TLS and B-cell content predict immunotherapy response across
melanoma, RCC and sarcoma (PMID 31942075, PMID 31942077), with T follicular helper
infiltration as an earlier prognostic hint (PMID 23778140). Induction is now achievable
preclinically — IL-33-activated ILC2s induce TLS in pancreatic cancer (PMID 39814891) —
and clinically approachable via Fc-optimised CD40 agonism, which elicits TLS formation
and systemic antitumour immunity in patients (PMID 40816292).

## Human evidence

CD8 infiltration is an independent favourable prognostic indicator (basal-like breast,
PMID 22420471), and the infiltration/PD-L1 cross-classification (PMID 25977340) is the
scheme that still organises trial stratification into hot/altered/cold tumours.
CXCR4 antagonism plus pembrolizumab and chemotherapy in PDAC (COMBAT/BL-8040,
PMID 32451495) is the main clinical test of blocking the exclusion route.

## What failed and why

CXCR4 blockade produced modest single-arm activity in PDAC and has not translated into
randomised benefit; the corpus suggests why — chemokine gradients are redundant, and
removing one exclusion signal does not create the CXCL9/10 gradient needed to import
effectors. The productive framing is that trafficking interventions must be paired with
a priming source (see notes/08, notes/09).

## Open question

TLS induction has no accepted pharmacological standard, no agreed maturity readout, and
unclear durability after the inducing agent stops.
