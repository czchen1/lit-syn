# CAFs, ECM and TGF-β-driven immune exclusion

## Mechanism

Desmoplastic stroma restricts immunotherapy in two ways: physically, by raising
interstitial pressure and collapsing vessels (hyaluronan/collagen), and
immunologically, via CAF-derived TGF-β, CXCL12 and antigen-presenting CAF states. The
corpus establishes CAF heterogeneity as the central fact: inflammatory (iCAF) versus
myofibroblastic (myCAF) populations in PDAC (PMID 28232471), antigen-presenting CAFs
(PMID 31197017), and a cross-tissue fibroblast atlas assigning distinct immune-modulatory
roles to myofibroblast subtypes (PMID 39303725).

## Human evidence

- FAP⁺ CAF-derived CXCL12 excludes T cells from PDAC and its blockade synergises with
  anti-PD-L1 — the single most cited mechanistic result in this axis (PMID 24277834).
- Spatial/single-cell work in patients links FAP⁺ fibroblasts to SPP1⁺ macrophages as a
  coordinated exclusion niche in colorectal cancer (PMID 35365629), and identifies a CAF
  subset restricting T-cell infiltration in HNSCC (PMID 37930937). CAF phenotype
  associates with outcome in NSCLC (PMID 38242124).
- Bifunctional anti-PD-L1/TGF-βRII trapping is the main clinical instrument here
  (PMID 32079617); galunisertib plus durvalumab in metastatic PDAC gives the phase 1b
  read on kinase-level TGF-β inhibition (PMID 33688022).
- Stromal remodelling plus stem-cell-plasticity targeting overcomes chemoresistance in
  TNBC (PMID 30042390).

## What failed and why

Enzymatic stromal ablation improved drug delivery in mice (PEGPH20-type approach,
PMID 22439937) but failed clinically, and the mechanistic reason is in this corpus:
depleting myofibroblasts/fibrosis outright *accelerates* PDAC and worsens immune
suppression (PMID 24856586). Stroma is partly restraining the tumour; the therapeutic
goal is normalisation, not removal.

## Open question

Which CAF state to target, with what selectivity. FAP is a marker of multiple states,
and no clinical agent in this corpus is subset-selective.
