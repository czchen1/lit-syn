# Cell therapy against the solid-tumour TME

## Why this belongs in a TME corpus

Adoptive cell therapy fails in solid tumours largely for TME reasons rather than
targeting reasons, so cell-therapy engineering has become a form of TME modulation
carried in the effector cell.

## What patients have shown

- HER2 CAR-T in sarcoma: persistence without adequate expansion or tumour control —
  the founding solid-tumour limitation (PMID 25800760).
- EGFRvIII CAR-T in glioblastoma: a single infusion drove antigen loss *and* induced
  adaptive resistance (Treg and inhibitory-molecule upregulation) in resected tumour —
  the single most instructive human cell-therapy TME result (PMID 28724573). Repeated
  infusion plus pembrolizumab showed no efficacy (PMID 38216766), confirming that neither
  redosing nor checkpoint blockade fixes it.
- Armouring works in humans: PSMA-targeting, TGF-β-insensitive CAR-T produced responses in
  mCRPC (phase 1, PMID 35314843) — direct evidence that neutralising one dominant TME
  signal changes clinical activity.
- TIL therapy: lifileucel monotherapy has activity in advanced NSCLC, including ICI-
  refractory disease (PMID 38563600).
- CAR-macrophages have entered the clinic for HER2⁺ solid tumours (PMID 39920391),
  building on human CAR-M preclinical work (PMID 32361713).

## Engineering strategies represented

Signalling rewiring (JAK-STAT-containing CAR, PMID 29400710), autocrine
immunometabolic support (IL-10-expressing CAR-T resisting dysfunction and clearing
metastatic solid tumours, PMID 38168996), transcriptional stemness enforcement (FOXO1,
PMID 38600376), and senolytic CAR-T targeting uPAR⁺ senescent cells — a non-tumour-cell
target within the microenvironment (PMID 32555459).

## Limits

Reviews converge on the same list: antigen heterogeneity and loss, poor trafficking,
exhaustion, and metabolic insufficiency in the TME (PMID 33824268, PMID 36717905,
PMID 40610404). Nothing here is a solved problem in solid tumours; the phase 1 signals
above are early and small.

## Open question

Whether cell-intrinsic armouring (TGF-β insensitivity, IL-10, FOXO1) can substitute for
modifying the host TME pharmacologically, or whether both are required — the corpus
currently supports "both" only by inference, not by comparative trial data.
