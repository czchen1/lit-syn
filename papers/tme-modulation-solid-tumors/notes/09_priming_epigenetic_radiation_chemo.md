# Priming the cold tumour: epigenetic, radiation and chemotherapy approaches

## Epigenetic priming

The mechanism is viral mimicry: DNA methyltransferase inhibition de-represses endogenous
retroviruses, generating dsRNA and a cell-intrinsic interferon response (PMID 26317466).
The corollary is that tumours actively silence retroelements to stay invisible — KDM5B
recruits SETDB1 to do exactly that, and its loss restores immunogenicity
(PMID 34671158); HIF-regulated ERV translation adds a hypoxia-linked route in RCC
(PMID 40023154). LSD1 ablation enables checkpoint blockade through the same dsRNA-stress
logic (PMID 29937226). HDAC inhibition is a separate, less clean mechanism — it raises
PD-1 ligands and augments PD-1 blockade (PMID 26297712).

Human evidence is phase 2-scale: epigenetic potentiation of NY-ESO-1 vaccination in
ovarian cancer (PMID 24535937); pembrolizumab plus entinostat in metastatic uveal
melanoma (PEMDAC, PMID 34453044); entinostat plus nivolumab in metastatic PDAC
(PMID 39532835). TNBC is the tumour type where the axis is most developed conceptually
(PMID 32461152, PMID 38719195, PMID 40155623), and EZH1/EZH2 inhibition is being used to
improve *adoptive* T cells rather than the tumour (PMID 39983725).

Why results are modest: class-wide epigenetic drugs act on T cells as well as tumour
cells, and the therapeutic window between de-repressing ERVs and impairing effector
differentiation is narrow.

## Radiation priming

Radiotherapy is the most clinically mature TME modulator here. Fractionated — not
single-dose — radiotherapy plus CTLA-4 blockade produces abscopal responses
(PMID 19706802), and this translated: radiotherapy induces responses to CTLA-4 blockade
in chemorefractory NSCLC (PMID 30397353), SBRT plus pembrolizumab improves response over
pembrolizumab alone in metastatic NSCLC (randomised phase 2, PMID 31294749), and
pembrolizumab plus radiotherapy induces systemic responses in immunologically cold NSCLC
(PMID 40696153). *Low-dose* radiotherapy reverses immune desertification and resistance —
a mechanistically distinct, infiltration-oriented use of radiation (PMID 34479871).

Negative anchors matter: ipilimumab after radiotherapy in mCRPC missed its primary
endpoint (PMID 24831977), and adding nivolumab to chemoradiotherapy in MGMT-unmethylated
newly diagnosed glioblastoma failed in phase 3 (CheckMate 498-type design,
PMID 35511454). Radiation also *creates* suppression — a post-radiotherapy TME with
recurrence-promoting features (PMID 26105538), so schedule and dose determine whether
radiation primes or immunosuppresses.

## Chemotherapy and immunogenic cell death (ICD)

ICD is the unifying concept: dying tumour cells release calreticulin/ATP/HMGB1 and
provide both antigen and adjuvant. The corpus covers nanoparticle-mediated ICD
(PMID 30016571, PMID 29675465), necroptosis-directed ICD (PMID 38454135) and PDT-induced
ICD (PMID 31842994). Human mechanistic depth comes from single-cell/spatial profiling of
pembrolizumab plus radiation in TNBC, which resolves three distinct response trajectories
rather than a binary outcome (PMID 38194915).

## Open question

Dose/fraction/timing optimisation is unsolved and is the main reason radio-immunotherapy
trials disagree; the corpus contains no head-to-head schedule comparison in humans.
