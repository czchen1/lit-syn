# Suppressive lymphoid compartment: Tregs and T-cell exhaustion

## Mechanism

Two distinct problems are often conflated. Tregs are an *active* suppressive population
recruited into tumours — the founding observation being CCR4/CCL22-driven Treg
recruitment in ovarian cancer with a survival penalty (PMID 15322536), later generalised
to the CD8:Treg ratio as the functional readout (PMID 16344461). Exhaustion is a
*cell-intrinsic differentiation state* of CD8 T cells, driven by chronic antigen and
enforced co-inhibitory signalling; the corpus supports co-inhibitory receptor
cooperativity rather than redundancy — Tim-3/PD-1 (PMID 20819927) and LAG-3/PD-1
synergising on the same cell to suppress autocrine IFN-γ (PMID 39121848).

## Human evidence

- Treg depletion improves vaccine-induced immunity in patients, the earliest clean human
  proof of the axis (PMID 16308572).
- CTLA-4 blockade shifts the intratumoral effector:Treg ratio in patients
  (PMID 18818309) — the mechanism now attributed largely to Fc-dependent Treg depletion.
- Anti-TIGIT improves PD-L1 blockade through *myeloid and Treg* effects rather than
  direct CD8 de-repression (PMID 38418879), an important reframing given the negative
  phase 3 TIGIT programmes.
- Fc-engineered CTLA-4 blockade (botensilimab) plus PD-1 shows activity in MSS
  colorectal cancer, the canonical checkpoint-refractory setting (phase 1,
  PMID 38871975).
- Neoadjuvant PARPi/chemotherapy in ovarian cancer nominates effector-Treg targeting
  specifically in homologous-recombination-deficient disease (PMID 38971151).

## What failed and why

PD-1 blockade can *amplify* PD-1⁺ Tregs and drive hyperprogression (PMID 31028147) —
the strongest mechanistic warning in this axis that de-repressing an inhibitory receptor
is not cell-type selective. Treg-selective targets (CCR8, TNFR2) are attractive for this
reason but are represented preclinically here, not clinically.

## Open question

Whether exhaustion is best addressed by receptor blockade at all, versus preserving
progenitor-exhausted/TCF1⁺ populations upstream — which links this axis to
`cell_therapy_tme` (FOXO1 stemness, PMID 38600376) rather than to checkpoint chemistry.
