# TME-directed delivery, regulated cell death, and physical/energy-based modulation

## TME-responsive delivery and regulated cell death

Two ideas are combined in this axis. The first is *stimulus-responsive delivery*: carriers
that decompose in the tumour's acidic, H₂O₂-rich, hypoxic milieu, exemplified by hollow
MnO₂ platforms that relieve hypoxia while delivering payload (PMID 29026068), and
reviewed as precision nanomedicine for TME navigation (PMID 40457437).

The second is *which death pathway* to induce, because the immunological consequences
differ. Ferroptosis is bidirectional in this corpus and that is its defining feature:
CD8 T cells kill in part by inducing tumour ferroptosis (PMID 31043744), yet ferroptosis
of tumour-infiltrating neutrophils causes immune suppression (PMID 36385526). Therapeutic
exploitation is therefore selectivity-dependent — tumour-specific GPX4 degradation in PDAC
(PMID 37910602), CPT1A inhibition synergising with immunotherapy in lung cancer
(PMID 38453925), ferroptosis heterogeneity guiding combinations in TNBC (PMID 36257316),
and a metastatic gastric cancer atlas linking ferroptosis to immunotherapy response
(PMID 39097198). Pyroptosis is the parallel route: mitochondria-targeted PDT triggering
GSDME-mediated pyroptosis sensitises anti-PD-1 in colorectal cancer (PMID 38429070), and
PARP inhibitors trigger TNF–caspase-8–GSDMD/E pyroptosis in ovarian cancer
(PMID 39366751) — a mechanism relevant to already-approved drugs.

## Physical and energy-based modulation

Energy deposition is a way to convert a cold tumour locally without systemic exposure.
Hyperthermia is the historical precedent (PMID 12147435); cryoablation plus CTLA-4
blockade is the cleanest preclinical proof of in-situ vaccination by physical means
(PMID 22108823). Photodynamic and photothermal therapy are the largest sub-literature
(PMID 33431631, PMID 37129253), including nanoscale coordination polymers combining
chemotherapy and PDT to potentiate checkpoint blockade (PMID 27530650); NIR
photoimmunotherapy has reached clinical use in HNSCC (PMID 31335117). Sonodynamic
therapy extends the same logic to depth (PMID 32337527, PMID 37914681, PMID 39735354),
and focused ultrasound has been used clinically for transcranial BBB opening and
immunostimulation in brain tumours (PMID 33547073, with a GTP-activatable
immuno-sonodynamic probe in PMID 39613729).

## Limits

Both axes are dominated by preclinical work with device- and formulation-specific
results, and depth/access constrain clinical use (light penetration for PDT, acoustic
window for SDT). The corpus contains no randomised evidence that adding energy-based
modulation to checkpoint blockade improves survival.

## Open question

Whether a physically primed single lesion produces reliable systemic (abscopal) immunity
— the same unresolved question as radiation priming in notes/09, with less clinical data.
