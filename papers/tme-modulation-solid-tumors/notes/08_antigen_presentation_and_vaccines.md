# Antigen presentation, MHC-I loss, IFN-γ pathway defects and neoantigen therapy

## Mechanism

This axis contains the least reversible resistance in the corpus, because the defects are
in the tumour cell's ability to be seen at all. Three classes appear:

1. **IFN-γ sensing loss** — JAK1/2 loss-of-function causes primary resistance to PD-1
   blockade (PMID 27903500).
2. **MHC-I loss** — genetic (B2M) and, importantly, *non-genetic*: PDAC degrades MHC-I
   through NBR1-dependent autophagy, which is pharmacologically restorable
   (PMID 32376951). Reviewed as a target class in PMID 38041084.
3. **Oncogenic-pathway-driven exclusion** — STK11/LKB1 deficiency drives neutrophil
   recruitment and suppressive cytokines (PMID 26833127) and predicts PD-1 inhibitor
   resistance in KRAS-mutant lung adenocarcinoma (PMID 29773717), the most actionable
   genotype-level resistance marker in routine practice.

Radiation is the counter-mechanism with the longest history: it broadens the presented
peptide repertoire and raises MHC-I (PMID 16636135), linking this axis to notes/09.

## Human evidence: neoantigen therapy

If antigen visibility is the problem, supplying antigen is the intervention. The corpus
tracks a clean progression: first-in-human personalised neoantigen vaccine in melanoma
(PMID 28678778); individualised mRNA-4157/V940 plus pembrolizumab improving
recurrence-free survival versus pembrolizumab in resected melanoma (randomised phase 2,
PMID 38246194); autogene-cevumeran-type RNA neoantigen vaccines priming T cells in
resected PDAC (PMID 37165196) with follow-up showing those CD8 clones are long-lived
(PMID 39972124); and a neoantigen vaccine generating immunity in RCC — a low-TMB tumour
(PMID 39910301).

## What failed and why

Nothing in this axis has a definitive negative phase 3 in the corpus, which is itself the
point: the field is early and the evidence is favourable but small-N. The structural risk
is that vaccination presumes intact presentation and IFN-γ signalling — precisely what is
broken in the resistance papers above. Patients selected for vaccine trials by TMB rather
than by presentation competence may be the wrong denominator.

## Open question

Whether MHC-I restoration (autophagy inhibition, epigenetic de-repression) should be a
prerequisite arm for neoantigen therapy in PDAC-like tumours.
