# Cancer therapies, clinical and preclinical, 2026-03-09 → 2026-09-09 — consolidated synthesis

A single end-to-end reference over the 18,424-record corpus indexed in `index.tsv`. It consolidates the modality notes (`notes/01`–`notes/09`) and adds cross-cutting analysis; methodology and caveats are in `notes/10`. Every PMID cited here resolves to a row in `index.tsv`; claims are abstract-level and should be verified against the linked record before reuse.

## Executive summary

- **The corpus is 42% clinical, 54% preclinical, 4% evidence synthesis.** Of 7,671 clinical records, 2,048 carry an explicit trial phase (739 phase 3, 930 phase 2, 379 phase 1); 773 of those are in tier 1–2 venues and constitute the practice-relevant core.
- **Immunotherapy is the largest clinical block, and its questions have changed.** Checkpoint blockade (2,200 records) is now about perioperative use, de-escalation, next-generation axes (LAG-3, TIGIT, CD40, CD73/adenosine), route (intratumoural, intrathecal), and on-treatment human resistance biology, not about whether PD-(L)1 works.
- **PD-1×VEGF bispecifics and CD3 engagers moved into first line.** Ivonescimab (HARMONi-A, *JAMA*, PMID 42307937), epcoritamab + R-CHOP (EPCORE NHL-2, *Blood*, PMID 42622258), teclistamab induction in newly diagnosed myeloma (*Nat Med*, PMID 42350642), and CAR-T in smouldering myeloma (CAR-PRISM, *Nat Med*, PMID 42010117) mark T-cell redirection's shift from salvage to front line.
- **RAS became a clinical target class.** Daraxonrasib phase 3 in pancreatic cancer (*NEJM*, PMID 42223072), HRS-4642 for G12D (*Nat Med*, PMID 42426224), and fulzerasib + cetuximab first-line NSCLC (KROCUS, *Lancet Oncol*, PMID 41926959) are the anchors; preclinical work is dominated by adaptive pathway reactivation.
- **Radiopharmaceuticals matured in prostate and NET and started on new targets.** PSMAddition (*Lancet*, PMID 42561994), COMPETE (*Lancet*, PMID 42392118), OCLURANDOM (*Lancet Oncol*, PMID 42225102), plus first FAP-targeted (LuMIERE, PMID 42223513) and ²²⁵Ac series.
- **Randomised radiotherapy evidence is mostly about doing less** — SBRT vs hypofractionated IMRT in prostate (*JAMA*, PMID 42593775), proton beam in oropharynx (TORPEdO, *Lancet*, PMID 41875914), response-adapted rectal organ preservation (*Lancet Oncol*, PMID 42633754), stereotactic vs whole-brain RT (*JAMA*, PMID 41712219).
- **Chemotherapy still generates most randomised evidence**, now steered by ctDNA (CIRCULATE, *Ann Oncol*, PMID 42225236; *Nat Med*, PMID 42260101) and locoregional delivery (CAIRO6, *Lancet Oncol*, PMID 42372745; IP paclitaxel, *JAMA Oncol*, PMID 42166134).
- **Preclinical work is about combination and resistance, not new monotherapy.** The recurring structure is a resistance node identified in human data → mouse/organoid/PDX validation → combination with an approved backbone. The largest preclinical masses (TME/metabolic 1,341, physical/ablative 953, nanomedicine 760) are heavily nanoparticle-enabled, subcutaneous-model literatures with immunogenic-cell-death endpoints of low clinical predictive value.
- **Delivery, not target, is the limiting step across RNA/gene therapy, cytokines, oncolytics and engagers.** Localising a potent-but-toxic agent (IL-12, CD3 engagers, CD40 agonists, CRISPR editors) recurs in every one of those buckets.

## Corpus and methods (short)

Europe PMC REST, `FIRST_PDATE:[2026-03-09 TO 2026-09-09]`, MEDLINE + PMC + preprints; 67 compound queries across 20 modality groups plus trial-vocabulary, preclinical-model, 58-journal and pediatric/CNS sweeps → 98,991 unique records. Rule-based curation (pubtype/title exclusions → cancer context → therapy context → evidence level → modality → disease/preprint tags → venue tier → title and DOI dedup) → 18,424 records. ## Part I — Clinical: what the window changed

### I.1 Immune checkpoint blockade (notes/04)
Perioperative intensification: adjuvant nivolumab in resected NSCLC (*JAMA*, PMID 42224490), nivolumab + CRT ± ipilimumab in stage III NSCLC (*Nat Cancer*, PMID 42129521), atezolizumab in early TNBC (NSABP B-59, *Nat Med*, PMID 42680950), cemiplimab + fianlimab in I-SPY2 (*JAMA Oncol*, PMID 42530945), perioperative tislelizumab in gastric cancer (*Cancer Cell*, PMID 42456653). Advanced disease: benmelstobart + anlotinib vs pembrolizumab (CAMPASS, *Lancet Oncol*, PMID 41825453), SKYSCRAPER-06 TIGIT (*JAMA Oncol*, PMID 42060297), durvalumab/tremelimumab ± lenvatinib with TACE (*Lancet Oncol*, PMID 42636832). Route: intratumoural anti-CTLA-4 (*Nature*, PMID 42056527), intrathecal nivolumab (*Nat Cancer*, PMID 42243276). New axes: sotigalimab (*Cancer Discov*, PMID 42013310), quemliclustat (*Nat Med*, PMID 41912809), cibisatamab + FAP-4-1BBL (*Nat Med*, PMID 42010119). Human-sample resistance biology (*Cancer Discov*, PMID 41511850, PMID 42013417; *Cell*, PMID 41999744) is the most transferable science in the bucket.

### I.2 T-cell redirection: CAR-T and engagers (notes/01, 02)
CAR-T earlier: smouldering myeloma (PMID 42010117), liso-cel + ibrutinib in CLL (*Blood*, PMID 42520199), bicistronic CD19/CD22 in pediatric ALL (*JAMA Oncol*, PMID 42690647). Construct innovation in phase 1: dual BCMA/GPRC5D (PMID 42166352, PMID 42413007), armoured GPC3 CAR-T in HCC (*Nature*, PMID 42457964), GPNMB CAR-T (*Nat Cancer*, PMID 42387022), intracranial B7-H3 CAR-T in GBM (*Nat Med*, PMID 42562965), base-edited off-the-shelf CAR33 (*Sci Transl Med*, PMID 42616838), **in vivo BCMA CAR generation** (*Nat Med*, PMID 41882404). Engagers: PD-1×VEGF class (PMID 42307937, PMID 42636833, PMID 42587052), epcoritamab and mosunetuzumab fixed-duration/first-line regimens (PMID 42622258, PMID 42285113, PMID 41862428), cevostamab (*Nat Med*, PMID 42487061), MAGE-A4 TCR-bispecifics (*Nat Med*, PMID 42219399), englumafusp + glofitamab (*Nat Med*, PMID 42463852). Class-level safety is now quantified: TRAE meta-analysis (*Lancet Oncol*, PMID 42285121), second primary malignancies after T-cell engagers (*JAMA Oncol*, PMID 42313425), decade-long CD19 CAR-T persistence (*Nat Med*, PMID 42575986).

### I.3 Antibody–drug conjugates (notes/03)
New targets with topoisomerase-I payloads: B7-H3 (I-DXd, *Lancet Oncol*, PMID 41926962), SEZ6 (ABBV-706, *Nat Med*, PMID 42225988), nectin-4 beyond urothelial (*Lancet Oncol*, PMID 42636836), EGFR (SYS6010, *Cancer Cell*, PMID 42594871), HER2 (HORIZON-Breast01, *Lancet Oncol*, PMID 42385760). Payload chosen for the driver: anti-PMEL ADC with Gq/11-inhibitor payload in GNAQ/GNA11-mutant melanoma (*Nat Med*, PMID 42443515). First-generation ADCs in combination and sequencing (Dato-DXd, *Cancer Cell*, PMID 41999747; T-DXd + durvalumab, *Nat Cancer*, PMID 42259988; EV + pembrolizumab 5-year, PMID 42155320). Resistance: endocytic evasion (*Cancer Cell*, PMID 42167230), antigen heterogeneity (*Cancer Discov*, PMID 41925564), in-vivo "click" rescue (*Nature*, PMID 42457957).

### I.4 Targeted small molecules (notes/06)

## Part II — Preclinical: recurring structures

1. **Resistance node → combination.** ADC endocytic evasion (PMID 42167230); RAS pathway reactivation in AML driving FLT3/BCL2-inhibitor resistance (*Blood*, PMID 42224378); TGF-β blockade causing hyperprogression in SCLC (*Cancer Discov*, PMID 42018154); CAR-T ferroptosis (*Nat Cancer*, PMID 42270901) and ATP–P2RY2–PGE2 adaptive resistance (*Immunity*, PMID 42392075); BTK-degrader resistance.
2. **Product/agent fitness rather than new binders.** GLUT3 CAR-T (PMID 41984929), NF-κB durability programs (*Blood*, PMID 42275255), NFIL3 dysfunction driver (*Cancer Discov*, PMID 41747243), ferritin-aggregation CAR avidity (*Cell*, PMID 41806835), logic-gated trispecific macrophage engagers (*Nat Biotechnol*, PMID 41826511), glycoengineered FcγRIIIa (*Immunity*, PMID 42034063).
3. **Localising potent-but-toxic agents.** IL-12/IL-15/IL-21 scaffolds, TLR/STING/CD40 agonist conjugates, mRNA-encoded cytokines/engagers/CARs, erythrocyte- and liposome-mediated in-situ CAR-myeloid programming (*Sci Transl Med*, PMID 41880522; *Nat Commun*, PMID 42285952), FAP-CAR mRNA LNPs (PMID 41686204), armed oncolytic vectors and engineered bacteria.
4. **Metabolism and stroma as immune levers.** Adenosine/purinergic axis (clinically most advanced), macrophage metabolic checkpoints (ALDH2–CXCL9, PMID 42168200), dietary sulfur amino acids → NKT–XCL1–cDC1 (*Immunity*, PMID 42190649), microbiota-derived UDP-galactose under low-protein diet (*Nat Cancer*, PMID 42608587), arginine metabolism in ARID1A-deficient TNBC (PMID 42191692), stromal barriers to antibody penetration (*Nat Biotechnol*, PMID 42236958).
5. **Radiochemistry and delivery chemistry.** ²²⁵Ac/²¹²Pb chelators, albumin binders, pretargeting; beyond-liver LNP tropism; circular and self-amplifying RNA; in-vivo CRISPR delivery; stimuli-responsive and biomimetic carriers. The clinical edge is thin; the vehicle characterisation is the transferable content.
6. **Nanoparticle-enabled physical therapy** (photothermal/photodynamic/sonodynamic/chemodynamic, ~750 preclinical records) is a materials literature with ICD readouts in subcutaneous models; read it as such.

## Part III — Cross-cutting observations

- **Chemotherapy is the comparator for most positive trials in other buckets** (RAS, ADC, engager, checkpoint); the `chemo_conventional` bucket is the control-arm context for the whole corpus.
- **ctDNA has become an interventional variable**, not just a biomarker: adjuvant chemotherapy triggers (colon, rectal), endocrine switching at ESR1 emergence, targeted→immune switching in BRAF melanoma (PMID 42168180), HPV ctDNA-adapted RT (PMID 42098120).
- **Earlier and gentler**: CAR-T in smouldering disease, daratumumab in high-risk MGUS (D-PRISM, PMID 41951609), ibrutinib in early CLL (CLL12, PMID 42322115), aspirin in Lynch syndrome (CaPP3, PMID 42425127), KRAS vaccines before cancer; alongside de-escalation in RT, transplant conditioning, and fixed-duration engager/BCL2 regimens.
- **Chinese sponsors dominate the first-line bispecific and next-generation checkpoint readouts**, and many `clinical_other` regimen comparisons; this matters for generalisability of populations and comparators.
- **Long-horizon safety is now a publication category of its own** — second malignancies after engagers, CAR-T persistence and late neurotoxicity, clonal hematopoiesis after Lu-PSMA (PMID 41649873), 15–20-year follow-ups of chemotherapy and endocrine trials.
- **Combination is the default preclinical claim**; monotherapy efficacy in a single subcutaneous syngeneic model is the modal preclinical paper and should be weighted accordingly.

## Caveats (see notes/10 for the full list)

Classification is regex-based on title + abstract; phase labels can be inherited from cited trials; a single primary modality undercounts combinations; tier is venue, not quality; Europe PMC misses conference abstracts and Embase-only journals; preprints (7%) are not peer-reviewed; 18k rows were not individually adjudicated, and only cited records were checked by hand. This is a literature index and synthesis, not a guideline.
