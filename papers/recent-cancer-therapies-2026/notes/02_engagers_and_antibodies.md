# 02 — T-cell engagers, bispecifics, and other antibodies

Buckets: `bispecific_tce` (T-cell/immune-cell engagers and bispecific/bifunctional antibodies) and `antibody_other` (naked monoclonals, fusion proteins, antibody-adjacent biology). `bispecific_tce` has the highest *proportion* of prospective trial reports of any bucket in the corpus — this is a modality being read out, not discovered.

## 1. PD-1×VEGF bispecifics are the story of the window

Ivonescimab (PD-1×VEGF) generated the highest-profile readouts: added to chemotherapy in EGFR-mutant NSCLC after progression (HARMONi-A, *JAMA*, PMID 42307937; *Lancet Oncol*, PMID 42636833), plus first-line biliary tract cancer with gem/cis (*J Hepatol*, PMID 42409321). A PD-1/VEGF bispecific plus chemotherapy in first-line TNBC (*Nat Med*, PMID 42587052) extends the class beyond lung. Cadonilimab (PD-1×CTLA-4) contributes cervical (COMPASSION subgroups, PMID 41826281), PD-L1-negative NSCLC (PMID 42285994) and endometrial (PMID 42000370) data. Retlirafusp alfa (PD-L1/TGF-β) was tested neoadjuvantly in stage III NSCLC (PMID 42303632).

The pattern: **dual-checkpoint or checkpoint-plus-stromal bispecifics are being positioned as chemotherapy partners in first line**, largely from Chinese sponsors, and increasingly against pembrolizumab-based standards rather than placebo.

## 2. CD3 engagers in hematology: fixed duration, first line, combinations

- **First-line and fixed-duration lymphoma.** Epcoritamab + R-CHOP in newly diagnosed high-IPI LBCL (EPCORE NHL-2, *Blood*, PMID 42622258); epcoritamab ± lenalidomide as first-line DLBCL (EPCORE DLBCL-3, *Lancet Haematol*, PMID 42285113); epcoritamab + R² in first-line follicular lymphoma (PMID 42660129); fixed-duration subcutaneous mosunetuzumab in R/R FL (PMID 41862428) and randomised SC mosunetuzumab + polatuzumab vs R-pola (PMID 42283231).
- **Myeloma engagers maturing.** Talquetamab 3-year MonumenTAL-1 follow-up (PMID 42561120); teclistamab-based induction in transplant-eligible newly diagnosed myeloma (*Nat Med*, PMID 42350642); cevostamab (FcRH5×CD3) phase 1 (*Nat Med*, PMID 42487061); pavurutamab (BCMA×CD3) (PMID 41950113); linvoseltamab vs real-world SOC (PMID 41881954, PMID 41922313); real-world elranatamab (PMID 41896552) and CMV reactivation under teclistamab (PMID 41916955).
- **Solid-tumour and TCR-based engagers.** MAGE-A4/A8 TCR-bispecific (*Nat Med*, PMID 42219399); IMC-C103C (MAGE-A4×CD3 ImmTAC) (PMID 42476725); HER2×CD3×CD28 trispecific SAR443216 (PMID 42705861); tarlatamab response prediction by circulating tumour cells (*Cancer Discov*, PMID 41532856).
- **Costimulatory and novel-axis bispecifics.** Englumafusp alfa (CD19-4-1BBL) + glofitamab (*Nat Med*, PMID 42463852); CD20×CD37 bifunctional antibody (PMID 42216090); CTLA-4×OX40 (ATOR-1015, PMID 42019086); PD-1×TIM-3 lomvastomig (PMID 42320980); SL-172154 (CD47 inhibitor + CD40 agonist Fc-fusion) with mirvetuximab or PLD (PMID 41951722).

## 3. Class-level safety is now being quantified

Two systematic analyses in this window are the most decision-relevant documents in the bucket: treatment-related adverse events across CD3 bispecifics (*Lancet Oncol* meta-analysis, PMID 42285121) and **second primary malignancies after T-cell-engaging bispecific therapy** (*JAMA Oncol*, PMID 42313425). Renal-impairment use (PMID 41927459) and BCMA-vs-GPRC5D target selection (PMID 42687264) are the practical companions.

## 4. Preclinical engager engineering

Trispecific and myeloid-redirecting formats dominate: a logic-gated trispecific engager for macrophage killing in solid tumours (*Nat Biotechnol*, PMID 41826511); patient-derived lymphoma spheroids to predict glofitamab resistance (*Blood*, PMID 41746860). The unifying problem is **restricting engager activity to the tumour** — logic gating, conditional activation, and local delivery (see notes/08 for mRNA-encoded engagers).

## 5. Naked antibodies: mature agents, sharper questions

`antibody_other` is largely HER2/EGFR/VEGF-era agents under long-term or mechanistic scrutiny: FeDeriCa final analysis of fixed-dose SC pertuzumab-trastuzumab (PMID 42537585); APHINITY secondary analyses including AI-scored TILs (*Lancet Oncol*, PMID 42636838) and benefit by ER/HER2 expression (PMID 41934626); nimotuzumab added to chemoradiotherapy in nasopharyngeal carcinoma (PMID 41916997); zolbetuximab (CLDN18.2) subgroup and safety analyses (PMID 42095986); FGFR2b prevalence for bemarituzumab (PMID 42190313).

Preclinically, the TGF-β axis produced the most surprising signal: convergent inactivation of TGF-β signalling in advanced pancreatic cancer (*Cancer Discov*, PMID 41650466) and **hyperprogression when tumour-intrinsic TGF-β signalling is blocked in SCLC** (*Cancer Discov*, PMID 42018154) — a cautionary result for TGF-β-directed bifunctionals. Delivery-side work shows conserved stromal barriers to therapeutic antibody penetration (*Nat Biotechnol*, PMID 42236958) and glycoengineering of FcγRIIIa to strengthen ADCC (*Immunity*, PMID 42034063).
