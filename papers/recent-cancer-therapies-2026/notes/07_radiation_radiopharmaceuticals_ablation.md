# 07 — Radiotherapy, radiopharmaceuticals, and physical/ablative therapy

Buckets: `radiotherapy` (second-largest *clinical* bucket after checkpoint), `radiopharm`, `physical_ablative` (almost entirely preclinical — photothermal/photodynamic/sonodynamic nanoplatforms).

## 1. External-beam radiotherapy: de-escalation, hypofractionation, and organ preservation

The randomised evidence in this window is unusually rich and mostly about doing *less*:

- **Brain metastases:** stereotactic radiation vs hippocampal-avoidance whole-brain RT (*JAMA*, PMID 41712219).
- **Prostate:** SBRT vs moderately hypofractionated IMRT for intermediate risk (*JAMA*, PMID 42593775); single-fraction SBRT (*JAMA Oncol*, PMID 42313395); 10-year SBRT outcomes (PMID 42106276); high-dose RT with long-term ADT (GETUG-AFU 18, *Lancet Oncol*, PMID 42636837); CAN-2409 gene-mediated cytotoxic immunotherapy with RT (phase 3, PMID 42225101).
- **Rectal organ preservation:** chemoradiotherapy vs short-course RT for response-adapted organ preservation (*Lancet Oncol*, PMID 42633754); boost-dose effect on organ preservation (PMID 42142103); dose-escalated vs standard neoadjuvant CRT 9-year results (PMID 42456783); irinotecan added to preoperative CRT (ARISTOTLE, PMID 42508427).
- **Breast:** internal mammary/medial supraclavicular irradiation (EORTC 22922/10925 unplanned analysis, PMID 42372743); SUPREMO 5-year QoL (PMID 42508432); proton vs photon regional nodal irradiation lung response (PMID 42526652) and acute side effects (PMID 42600707).
- **Head and neck:** TORPEdO proton beam therapy for oropharyngeal cancer (phase 3, *Lancet*, PMID 41875914); de-intensification after TORS (PMID 41999781); HPV ctDNA risk-adapted therapy (ReACT 1.0, PMID 42098120); reduced-dose RT in EBV-DNA-selected nasopharyngeal carcinoma (PMID 42150706); superselective intra-arterial CRT for maxillary sinus (JCOG1212, PMID 42497481).
- **Lung:** one vs three SBRT fractions long-term (PMID 41833909); consolidative thoracic RT with atezolizumab in ES-SCLC (TREASURE, *JAMA Oncol*, PMID 42424047); immune-sparing SBRT randomised phase 2 (PMID 42486309); reduced thoracic RT for older/frail stage III (PMID 42201929); proton vs IMRT long-term for locally advanced NSCLC (PMID 42173326).
- **CNS and sarcoma:** intraoperative RT dose escalation in GBM (INTRAGO-II, *Lancet Oncol*, PMID 42372746); MRI-guided adaptive RT in high-grade glioma (UNITED, PMID 42134380); DOREMY dose reduction in myxoid liposarcoma (*JAMA Oncol*, PMID 42141895); 30-year proton outcomes in skull-base chondrosarcoma (PMID 41414803); reduced-dose RT without chemotherapy for intracranial germinoma (PMID 42706991).
- **Kidney:** SABR for primary kidney cancer long-term (FASTRACK II, *Lancet Oncol*, PMID 42144018).
- **Neoadjuvant RT–immunotherapy:** SBRT with durvalumab + oleclumab in ER+ breast cancer (*Nat Med*, PMID 42350643).

Pediatric: post-irradiation chemotherapy in ependymoma (COG, PMID 41423745); reduced-dose RT in germinoma (PMID 42706991). Note that neurocognitive-protection trials (e.g. memantine during cranial RT) are excluded by the curation rules as supportive care.

## 2. Radiopharmaceuticals: PSMA and SSTR mature, FAP and alpha emitters arrive

- **PSMA:** [¹⁷⁷Lu]Lu-PSMA-617 in hormone-sensitive disease (PSMAddition phase 3, *Lancet*, PMID 42561994); BULLSEYE in oligometastatic HSPC (*Lancet Oncol*, PMID 41926960); PRINCE with pembrolizumab (*Lancet Oncol*, PMID 41926961); retreatment (PMID 41679923); dose-intensified vs standard (PMID 42532645); concurrent with EBRT+ADT in N1 disease (PROQURE-I, PMID 42201380); neoadjuvant PSMA-I&T before prostatectomy (PMID 41931162); early PSMA upregulation predicting response to enzalutamide ± Lu-PSMA (*Nat Cancer*, PMID 41986500); clonal hematopoiesis after Lu-PSMA (PMID 41649873); LASER phase 2 in RCC (PMID 41545068).
- **Alpha emitters:** [²²⁵Ac]Ac-PSMA-617 vs Lu-PSMA rechallenge vs chemotherapy (PMID 42503230); 225Ac-PSMA-CY313 (PMID 42611148); [²²⁵Ac]Ac-DOTATATE vs salvage Lu-DOTATATE (PMID 42302403).
- **SSTR:** OCLURANDOM Lu-DOTATATE vs sunitinib in pancreatic NET (*Lancet Oncol*, PMID 42225102); COMPETE Lu-edotreotide vs everolimus (phase 3, *Lancet*, PMID 42392118); Lu-DOTATATE + octreotide in newly diagnosed G2–3 GEP-NET (PMID 42604001); CONTROL NETS PRRT + CAPTEM (PMID 42142431); Lu-DOTATATE/capecitabine randomised (PMID 41591997); intra-arterial vs IV PRRT (PMID 42169550).
- **FAP and new targets:** [¹⁷⁷Lu]Lu-FAP-2286 (LuMIERE, PMID 42223513); 3BP-3940 with Lu/Y/Ac (PMID 42225357); RTX-2358 (PMID 41786480); hypoxia-targeting 64Cu-ATSM in brain tumours (STAR-64, PMID 42431136); CAIX theranostics in ccRCC (PMID 42559379).
- **Radium-223 combinations:** with olaparib (PMID 42096662) and with cabozantinib in RCC bone metastases (RADICAL, PMID 42507969).

Preclinical radiopharm work is dominated by **new chelators/linkers for ²²⁵Ac and ²¹²Pb**, albumin-binding and pretargeting strategies to fix dosimetry, and BNCT/¹⁶¹Tb Auger-emitter exploration.

## 3. Physical and ablative therapy

Clinically thin but real: microwave ablation for localised prostate cancer (VIOLETTE, PMID 41810596); RFA for bone metastases (SPARTA, PMID 41915051); cryoablation for early breast cancer (FIRST, PMID 42157305); irreversible electroporation with RT in prostate (RTIRE, PMID 42517770) and its immune remodelling atlas (PMID 42128518); TACE + thermal ablation in HCC (TORCH phase 3, *JAMA Oncol*, PMID 42530948, filed under `chemo_conventional`); photodynamic therapy for dysphagia in esophageal cancer (PMID 42700165); fluorescence-guided surgery (cRGD-ZW800, PMID 42173866; PSMA-914, PMID 42559208).

The preclinical `physical_ablative` mass — ~750 papers — is nanoparticle-enabled photothermal, photodynamic, sonodynamic, chemodynamic and magnetic hyperthermia therapy, overwhelmingly in subcutaneous mouse models. It overlaps heavily with `nanomedicine_delivery` and should be read as a materials-science literature with immunogenic-cell-death readouts rather than as a therapeutic pipeline.
