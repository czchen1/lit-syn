# 06 — Targeted small molecules: kinase, RAS/MAPK, hematologic, DDR, degraders, epigenetic, endocrine

Buckets: `targeted_kinase` (largest preclinical bucket), `targeted_ras_mapk`, `targeted_heme`, `targeted_ddr`, `degrader`, `epigenetic`, `endocrine`. Together they are roughly a third of the corpus. The clinical edge is thick in hematology, lung and breast; the preclinical mass is dominated by resistance mechanism and combination logic.

## 1. RAS is now a clinical target class, not a target

The window contains the first mature clinical readouts of RAS(ON) and mutant-selective inhibitors beyond G12C:

- **Pan-RAS(ON):** daraxonrasib vs chemotherapy in previously treated metastatic pancreatic cancer (phase 3, *NEJM*, PMID 42223072) and the phase 1 report (*NEJM*, PMID 42090791); acquired-resistance analysis guiding rational combinations (*Nat Med*, PMID 42581230).
- **G12D:** HRS-4642 + chemotherapy in KRAS-G12D pancreatic cancer (phase 1b/2, *Nat Med*, PMID 42426224); RNK08954 preclinical-to-clinical (*Cancer Discov*, PMID 41557983).
- **G12C combinations:** fulzerasib + cetuximab first-line NSCLC (KROCUS, *Lancet Oncol*, PMID 41926959); ifebemtinib (FAK) + garsorasib in colorectal cancer (*Lancet Oncol*, PMID 42636835); sotorasib + panitumumab + FOLFIRI (PMID 42617424); olomorasib pan-tumour first-in-human (PMID 41820335); KRAS-G12V TCR gene therapy in pancreatic cancer (PMID 41814655) bridges to cell therapy.
- **BRAF/MEK:** dabrafenib + trametinib in differentiated thyroid cancer (phase 3, *Lancet Oncol*, PMID 42442381); adjuvant encorafenib + binimetinib in stage II melanoma (COLUMBUS-AD, PMID 42501621); COMBI-I long-term OS (PMID 42585631); ctDNA-guided switch from targeted to immune therapy in BRAF melanoma (PMID 42168180); encorafenib + cetuximab + FOLFIRI in BRAF-V600E colorectal cancer (PMID 42219860).
- **Preclinical:** the recurring theme is *adaptive RAS pathway reactivation* — in pancreatic models on RAS(ON) inhibitors, and even in AML where dynamic RAS activation drives FLT3/BCL2-inhibitor resistance (*Blood*, PMID 42224378).

## 2. Kinase inhibitors: sequencing and MRD-guided use

- **EGFR/ALK/ROS1/MET lung:** osimertinib ± chemotherapy in EGFR/TP53-co-mutant NSCLC (*JAMA*, PMID 42574006); aumolertinib ± chemotherapy (AENEAS2, *Lancet Oncol*, PMID 42296979); adjuvant alectinib QoL/safety (ALINA, PMID 42636834); adjuvant icotinib 3-year (ICWIP, PMID 42660909); mefatinib vs gefitinib (PMID 42586967); MRD in resected EGFR-mutant disease (PMID 42706268); ELIOS molecular profiling on first-line osimertinib (*Cancer Discov*, PMID 41790042); brain-penetrant ROS1 inhibitor JYP0322 against G2032R (*Cancer Cell*, PMID 42030931); BLU-451 for uncommon EGFR mutations (PMID 42126003); savolitinib in MET-amplified gastric cancer (*Nat Med*, PMID 42225990).
- **CDK4/6:** first-line vs second-line CDK4/6 inhibitor OS (*JAMA Oncol*, PMID 41712229); bireociclib + fulvestrant (BRIGHT-2, PMID 41854603); fovinaciclib first-line (PMID 42224659); dalpiciclib in visceral crisis (*Nat Cancer*, PMID 42481711); dose-reduced palbociclib pharmacodynamics (TBCRC 035, PMID 41812623).
- **FGFR and HIF-2α:** infigratinib vs gem/cis in FGFR2-fusion cholangiocarcinoma (phase 3, PMID 41850040) and in FGFR2-amplified gastric cancer (PMID 42337043); FGFR inhibition in SDH-deficient GIST (*Nat Med*, PMID 42191879); casdatifan (HIF-2α) durable responses in kidney cancer (*Nature*, PMID 42386974).
- **HER2 CNS disease:** tucatinib-trastuzumab-capecitabine for leptomeningeal metastasis (TBCRC049, *Nat Cancer*, PMID 41851506).
- **Pediatric CNS:** targeted therapies + RT in DIPG (BIOMEDE, *Nat Med*, PMID 42032072).

## 3. Hematologic targeted therapy: venetoclax everywhere, BTK next generation

- **CLL:** fixed-duration venetoclax-obinutuzumab vs FCR/BR (CRISTALLO, *Blood*, PMID 41770817); CLL13/GAIA 5-year (PMID 41911073); ibrutinib in early-stage CLL (CLL12, PMID 42322115); a decade of ibrutinib with/without TP53 aberration (PMID 41405491); orelabrutinib vs chemoimmunotherapy (PMID 42402625); first-line ibrutinib + venetoclax in older/TP53-mutant MCL (*Blood*, PMID 42462092); pirtobrutinib 5-year in WM (PMID 42069409); **pan-resistance to BTK degraders and inhibitors** (*Cancer Discov*, PMID 42484277) — a structural warning for the whole class.
- **AML/MDS:** OPTI-AML 28 vs 14 days venetoclax (*Blood*, PMID 42520193); ziftomenib + ven/aza in NPM1-mutant AML (PMID 42227701); pediatric venetoclax combinations (PMID 42419338); gilteritinib + azacitidine (PMID 42687805); dasatinib in CBF-AML (phase 3, PMID 41490515); quizartinib combinations (PMID 41942459, PMID 42374628); navitoclax + ruxolitinib in myelofibrosis (TRANSFORM-1, PMID 42378247); low-dose pediatric AML induction non-inferiority (PMID 41812214).
- **Myeloma:** isatuximab quadruplets (IMROZ MRD dynamics, PMID 41758929; Isa-KRd, *Nat Med*, PMID 41942646); KRd vs R after ASCT (PMID 41831471); daratumumab in high-risk MGUS/low-risk SMM (D-PRISM, PMID 41951609); Dara-CVRd induction/extended consolidation (PMID 42705255).
- **Lymphoma:** RELEVANCE 10-year (PMID 41915772); venetoclax + DA-EPOCH-R in double-hit lymphoma (PMID 42660130); PET-guided targeted therapy in Hodgkin (COBRA, PMID 42069408); tucidinostat + R-CHOP in double-expressor DLBCL (phase 3, *JAMA*, PMID 42018318, filed under `epigenetic`).

## 4. DNA damage response

The window is mostly PARP-inhibitor positioning: intensified chemotherapy vs standard-then-olaparib in HRD stage III breast cancer (*Lancet Oncol*, PMID 42372741); pembrolizumab + olaparib in HRD pancreatic cancer (POLAR, *Nat Med*, PMID 41882405); PARP + AR inhibition + ADT in metastatic prostate cancer (*NEJM*, PMID 42223064); niraparib neoadjuvant vs chemotherapy in HRD ovarian cancer (PMID 42623963); olaparib in BAP1/BARD1/BRIP1/PALB2-mutant cancers (PMID 42105598); adjuvant olaparib long-term update (PMID 42636977); resistance to neoadjuvant talazoparib via BRN2–ATR/STAT3 or SHLD2 subclones (PMID 41961819); ATR inhibition (ceralasertib) in ARID1A-deficient tumours (PMID 41686845); adavosertib (WEE1) in BRCA-mutant tumours (NCI-MATCH, PMID 42308463). Preclinical DDR work centres on PARP/ATR/WEE1/POLθ combinations with radiation and immunotherapy and on reversion-mutation surveillance.

## 5. Degraders and epigenetic drugs

`degrader` is the smallest bucket and almost entirely preclinical (PROTACs and molecular glues against BTK, AR, ER, BCL6, IKZF, KRAS, transcription factors) — the clinical signal is the BTK-degrader resistance paper above and the arrival of MDM2 antagonists (alrizomadlin in TP53-wild-type salivary gland cancer, PMID 41857036; ALRN-6924 in pediatrics, PMID 42189874).

`epigenetic` clinical entries: tazemetostat basket in SWI/SNF-altered solid tumours (PMID 41882006); abexinostat in R/R FL (PMID 42036411); tucidinostat with metronomic capecitabine + endocrine therapy (PMID 41986320); dordaviprone in diffuse midline glioma — a point/counterpoint on the approval (PMID 42172613) and post-HCT maintenance (PMID 42359621); bomedemstat (LSD1) in myelofibrosis (PMID 42054549); ABBV-744 (BET) (PMID 41843794); mutant-p53 reactivation with demethylation in AML/MDS (*Sci Transl Med*, PMID 42585289).

## 6. Endocrine therapy

- **Breast:** switching to camizestrant at ESR1-mutation emergence (*Lancet Oncol*, PMID 42442380) is the flagship — ctDNA-triggered switching before radiographic progression; ipatasertib + ET + CDK4/6i (PMID 42061370); fulvestrant vs capecitabine maintenance (PMID 42168151); INAVO120 safety (PMID 42202490); gedatolisib + fulvestrant ± palbociclib (VIKTORIA-1, PMID 41802242); SOFT/TEXT final outcomes (PMID 42229584); tamoxifen 20-year benefit by luminal subtype (PMID 41712503); neoadjuvant palbociclib + ET vs chemotherapy (PMID 41951647).
- **Prostate:** perioperative apalutamide (*NEJM*, PMID 42223077); transdermal estradiol patches (*NEJM*, PMID 41880608); high-dose RT with long-term ADT (GETUG-AFU 18, *Lancet Oncol*, PMID 42636837); SBRT + abiraterone in oligometastatic CRPC (ARTO, PMID 42372744); darolutamide by disease volume (PMID 42586872); deutenzalutamide after docetaxel/abiraterone (PMID 41968137); low- vs standard-dose abiraterone (PMID 42024845); enzalutamide + metformin (PMID 41862335); lifestyle mitigation of ADT effects (STAMINA, *Lancet Oncol*, PMID 42697217).

## Reading guidance

Kinase and RAS preclinical papers cite IC50/combination indices that rarely translate; prioritise those with PDX or GEMM in-vivo data (tagged `in_vivo` in `topics`) and human-sample resistance analyses. Hematology trial results here are often long-term follow-ups of already-approved regimens — valuable for durability, not for novelty.
