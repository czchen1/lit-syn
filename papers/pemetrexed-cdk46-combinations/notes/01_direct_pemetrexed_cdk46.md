# Direct pemetrexed + CDK4/6 inhibitor evidence

This is the core of the query: studies that actually combine pemetrexed with a CDK4/6 inhibitor. The dataset is small — one dedicated early-phase clinical trial, one clinical trial that permitted concurrent pemetrexed, and two preclinical studies — but internally consistent.

## Clinical

### Kim et al. 2018 — Abemaciclib + pemetrexed, Phase Ib, metastatic NSCLC (PMID 30082474)
*Clin Cancer Res.* The only dedicated clinical evaluation of a pemetrexed + CDK4/6i combination. A multicenter, nonrandomized, open-label Phase Ib tested twice-daily oral abemaciclib (150/200 mg) plus **pemetrexed** (per label), gemcitabine, or ramucirumab in stage IV NSCLC (86 patients total).
- **No abemaciclib MTD was identified** for the pemetrexed or gemcitabine arms (an MTD was defined only for the ramucirumab arm).
- **No clinically meaningful drug–drug PK interaction**: abemaciclib did not alter pemetrexed exposure and vice versa.
- **Disease control rate 57%** in the abemaciclib–pemetrexed arm (vs. 25% abemaciclib–gemcitabine, 54% abemaciclib–ramucirumab).
- Most common TEAEs: fatigue, diarrhea, neutropenia, decreased appetite, nausea — tolerable and consistent with the component drugs.
- **Interpretation:** establishes feasibility and pharmacologic compatibility of abemaciclib + pemetrexed in NSCLC, but is not powered for efficacy and never advanced to a randomized combination readout in NSCLC.

### Sahebjam et al. 2026 — Abemaciclib in NSCLC/melanoma brain metastases, Phase II (PMID 41768125)
*Neurooncol Adv.* Simon two-stage Phase II (NCT02308020) of BBB-penetrant abemaciclib in brain metastases. NSCLC patients could receive abemaciclib 150 mg BID **with concurrent pemetrexed or gemcitabine** (or 200 mg BID monotherapy).
- No confirmed intracranial responses in either the NSCLC (n=28) or melanoma (n=23) cohort; intracranial clinical benefit rate 26.1% (NSCLC).
- Median OS/PFS 7.1/1.6 months (NSCLC).
- **Interpretation:** pemetrexed was permitted alongside abemaciclib but the trial was not designed to isolate the combination effect; the result is a negative signal for abemaciclib activity in this CNS setting rather than an evaluation of pemetrexed synergy.

## Preclinical

### Ke et al. 2022 — Ribociclib + pemetrexed, lung adenocarcinoma (PMID 35686110)
*Front Oncol.* Direct preclinical test of ribociclib + pemetrexed in LUAD, motivated explicitly by the disappointing single-agent CDK4/6i results in lung cancer.
- Combination gave **significantly stronger antitumor effect than either agent alone** in vitro and in xenografts.
- **Complementary cell-cycle mechanism:** pemetrexed blocks cells in S phase; ribociclib arrests them in G1 — the combination captures both compartments.
- Ribociclib **strongly enhanced pemetrexed's pro-apoptotic activity via caspase/Bcl-2 signaling**.
- First report that the combination also **inhibits migration and invasion** of LUAD cells.
- **Interpretation:** the cleanest positive proof-of-concept that pemetrexed + a CDK4/6i is synergistic when the cell-cycle effects are complementary.

### Terenziani et al. 2022 — Abemaciclib + cisplatin/pemetrexed, pleural mesothelioma (PMID 36497412)
*Cancers.* Tests abemaciclib added to the standard MPM chemotherapy doublet (cisplatin + pemetrexed) in two MPM cell lines and a primary culture.
- CDKN2A/ARF loss (≈70% of MPM) provides the target rationale.
- **Abemaciclib + cisplatin + pemetrexed was more antiproliferative than chemotherapy alone** across all models.
- Mechanism was cell-type-dependent: cellular **senescence** (p21 induction) or **autophagic cell death** (AKT/mTOR impairment).
- The effect was **irreversible** — no proliferation resumed after drug withdrawal.
- Explicitly framed as consistent with the positive **MiST2** abemaciclib arm in mesothelioma patients.
- **Interpretation:** provides the strongest disease-specific rationale for a pemetrexed-containing CDK4/6i combination, pointing to mesothelioma (CDKN2A-null, pemetrexed-backbone) as the most logical clinical setting.

## Synthesis of the direct evidence

- The combination is **feasible and pharmacologically compatible** (Kim 2018) and **preclinically synergistic** in the two settings tested (LUAD, MPM).
- No randomized trial has tested pemetrexed + a CDK4/6i for efficacy.
- The biology favors **CDKN2A/p16-deleted, RB-proficient tumors** (mesothelioma; KRAS-mutant/p16-lost NSCLC subsets) and favors **schedule-aware dosing** to avoid the G1-arrest/S-phase-antimetabolite antagonism documented in the mechanism note.
