# Repurposed drugs, natural products & delivery-enabled combinations

A smaller, exploratory class of low-cost or off-patent agents and formulation strategies that potentiate pemetrexed. Evidence is almost entirely preclinical, but several are attractive because of favorable safety and mechanistic complementarity.

## Metabolic repurposing
- **Metformin synergizes with pemetrexed** in NSCLC, suppressing proliferation and invasion (combination index < 1) with enhanced apoptosis (Zhang et al. 2017, PMID 28719077). Rationale: AMPK/mTOR modulation and metabolic stress atop nucleotide depletion.

## Cell-death reprogramming
- **Pemetrexed sensitizes cisplatin therapy by inducing ferroptosis** in NSCLC cells (Wang et al. 2025, PMID 41646938) — connecting the antifolate mechanism to iron-dependent, non-apoptotic death and offering a way to overcome apoptosis-resistant disease.

## Natural products / chaperone targeting
- **Triptolide / minnelide suppress HSP70 and inhibit growth** in xenografts, complementing pemetrexed's stress mechanism (PMID 26000097).
- Additional natural-product sensitizers (e.g. Trichosanthes extract with cisplatin/pemetrexed) appear in the signaling class.

## Delivery-enabled combinations
- Nanocarrier and multi-drug delivery systems (e.g. crosslinked alginate/lactoferrin nanohydrogels for sequential triple-drug delivery, PMID 36365222; oral nanoemulsive systems) are catalogued as ways to **co-deliver and sequence** pemetrexed with partners, controlling schedule-dependent synergy pharmaceutically.

## Practical implications
1. **Metformin and ferroptosis inducers** are the most translationally interesting repurposing leads given safety and mechanistic fit.
2. Delivery/formulation approaches matter because much pemetrexed synergy is **schedule-dependent**, and co-formulation can enforce optimal sequencing.
3. This class is hypothesis-generating; none is practice-changing, and effect sizes need validation beyond cell-line/xenograft models.
