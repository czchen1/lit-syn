# Immunotherapy — anti-PD-(L)1 + pemetrexed/platinum

The single most important clinically validated pemetrexed combination beyond the platinum doublet is **anti-PD-1/PD-L1 immunotherapy added to pemetrexed + platinum**. Chemotherapy contributes immunogenic cell death, antigen release, and modulation of the tumor immune microenvironment that complements checkpoint blockade — a clinically synergistic (not merely additive) effect.

## Landmark trial
- **KEYNOTE-189 — Gandhi et al. 2018 (PMID 29658856), NEJM.** Pembrolizumab + pemetrexed + platinum vs. pemetrexed + platinum in metastatic nonsquamous NSCLC: significant improvement in overall survival and PFS across PD-L1 strata, establishing chemo-immunotherapy as first-line standard. This is the reference combination the rest of the IO literature builds on.

## Extensions and real-world / subgroup evidence (selected)
- **Five-year outcomes** of pembrolizumab-based therapy vs chemotherapy in Chinese NSCLC patients (PMID 41474069) — durability of the chemo-IO benefit.
- **Older patients / PD-L1 subgroups** — pembrolizumab + pemetrexed in older nonsquamous NSCLC (PMID 40007549).
- **Novel IO backbones on chemo** — tiragolumab (anti-TIGIT) + atezolizumab + chemotherapy in nonsquamous NSCLC (PMID 42060297); atezolizumab + chemotherapy real-world practice (PMID 41378717, 42187584).
- **Maintenance strategies** combining pemetrexed maintenance with IO (PMID 41808717).

## Mechanistic/translational threads
- The collection includes preclinical work on how pemetrexed and platinum reshape the immune microenvironment (T-cell priming, immunogenic cell death) to rationalize the clinical synergy.

## Practical implications
1. **Chemo-IO is the default first-line synergistic combination** in nonsquamous NSCLC; pemetrexed/platinum is the chemotherapy partner of choice.
2. Benefit is broad across PD-L1 expression but magnitude varies — PD-L1 remains a quantitative, not qualitative, selector.
3. Active frontiers: next-generation checkpoints (TIGIT), optimal maintenance (pemetrexed ± IO), and biomarker-refined patient selection.
