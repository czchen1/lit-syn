# Folate pathway, thymidylate synthase & transport modulation

Because pemetrexed *is* an antifolate, the most direct way to synergize with it is to manipulate the folate/one-carbon machinery, its target thymidylate synthase (TS), its polyglutamation (FPGS), or its cellular transport (PCFT/RFC). This class contains both **sensitizers** (lower TS, raise transport/activation) and mechanistic studies of **resistance** that reveal reversal strategies.

## Thymidylate synthase down-regulation sensitizes
- **Astaxanthin down-regulates TS via reduced MKK1/2–ERK1/2 signaling and enhances pemetrexed cytotoxicity** in NSCLC (Liao et al. 2016, PMID 27693704) — high TS is a canonical pemetrexed-resistance marker, so anything lowering it sensitizes.
- Multiple studies link **MEK/ERK signaling to TS expression**, connecting the MAPK note to antifolate sensitivity.

## Transport and delivery (PCFT / RFC)
- **Hypoxia lowers PCFT and confers pemetrexed resistance in mesothelioma**; LDH-A inhibition is explored to counter the hypoxic-glycolytic phenotype (Li Petri et al. 2020, PMID 32493992).
- Transport competence (PCFT/RFC) is repeatedly a determinant of intracellular pemetrexed accumulation and thus of combination efficacy.

## One-carbon metabolism nodes
- **MTHFD2 blockade enhances chemo/radiation efficacy** and is a targetable one-carbon node (Shukla et al. 2020, PMID 33262939).
- **L-leucovorin metabolism modulates antifolate toxicity and 5-FU/TS interactions** (Peters et al. 2024, PMID 39234107) — folate cofactor supply directly tunes antifolate activity, informing rescue vs potentiation.

## Resistance biology → reversal strategies
- **BMI1-mediated pemetrexed resistance** couples to SP1 activation and cancer stemness/EMT, nominating BMI1/stemness targeting to restore sensitivity (Shen et al. 2020, PMID 32726929).
- Additional records characterize FPGS/polyglutamation, TS amplification, and salvage-pathway (nucleoside) escape as reversible resistance mechanisms.

## Practical implications
1. **TS is both a resistance biomarker and a synergy lever** — combinations that suppress TS (via MEK/ERK or otherwise) sensitize to pemetrexed.
2. **PCFT/RFC transport and hypoxia** gate intracellular drug levels; targeting the hypoxic/glycolytic phenotype is a rational co-strategy in MPM.
3. Folate-cofactor handling (leucovorin) can either **rescue toxicity or modulate efficacy**, so cofactor scheduling matters in combination design.
