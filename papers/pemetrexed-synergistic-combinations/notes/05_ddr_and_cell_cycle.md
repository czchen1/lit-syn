# DNA-damage response & cell-cycle checkpoint inhibitors + pemetrexed

This is the most mechanistically coherent preclinical synergy class. Pemetrexed depletes nucleotides and induces replication stress and DNA damage; cancer cells survive by activating **S and G2/M checkpoints** (ATR–CHK1, WEE1) and DNA-repair pathways (base-excision repair, PARP-mediated repair, Fanconi/BRCA). Inhibiting these lets damaged cells proceed into mitotic catastrophe — reliably synergistic, and usually **schedule-dependent** (chemotherapy-induced damage first, then checkpoint abrogation).

## WEE1 (G2/M checkpoint)
- **CRISPR kinome screen nominates WEE1** as a combination target for cisplatin/pemetrexed in MPM; WEE1 inhibition overrides chemo-induced G2/M arrest and drives cell death in vitro and in vivo (Xu et al. 2020, PMID 31694888).
- **MK-1775 (adavosertib) + cisplatin** abrogates the G2/M checkpoint and sensitizes mesothelioma across histotypes (Indovina et al. 2014, PMID 24365782).
- **MK-1775 + gemcitabine** synergy in NSCLC (Chen et al. 2024, PMID 39605823).
- **Clinical translation** — two Phase II trials of adavosertib with docetaxel or carboplatin/pemetrexed (PMID 41212474), moving WEE1 inhibition into pemetrexed-backbone combinations.

## CHK1 / ATR (S and G2/M checkpoints)
- **CHK1 inhibition (AZD7762) sensitizes p53-mutant NSCLC to gemcitabine, pemetrexed, and radiotherapy**; CHK1 protein level marks sensitization (Grabauskiene et al. 2014, PMID 24418519).
- ATR/CHK1 inhibitors recur as replication-stress-exploiting partners for antimetabolite chemotherapy.

## PARP and base-excision repair
- **NAMPT inhibition (GMX1777/8) synergizes with pemetrexed** via PARP activation and enhanced NAD consumption, exploiting the folate-pathway/DNA-repair link (Chan et al. 2014, PMID 25145669).
- **Methoxyamine (BER inhibitor) enhances pemetrexed-mediated radiosensitization** (Bhattacharyya et al. 2016, PMID 27838149; see also radiosensitization note).
- PARP inhibitors (olaparib and others) and DDR-directed agents appear as sensitizers, with veliparib + carboplatin/paclitaxel providing a clinical DDR-combination benchmark (PMID 35331641).

## Practical implications
1. **WEE1 and CHK1/ATR inhibitors are the highest-conviction next-generation partners** for the pemetrexed/platinum backbone, especially in checkpoint-reliant, p53-mutant, or MPM tumors.
2. **Sequence chemotherapy → checkpoint inhibitor** to first generate the damage the checkpoint would otherwise repair.
3. p53 status and baseline checkpoint-kinase expression are candidate predictive biomarkers.
