# Antiangiogenic agents + pemetrexed

Antiangiogenics add an orthogonal mechanism (tumor-vasculature normalization / VEGF blockade) to the pemetrexed/platinum backbone. The clinical rationale is vascular normalization improving drug delivery plus direct antitumor effects; the combination is used both in induction and, notably, in **maintenance** where pemetrexed + bevacizumab is a well-studied doublet.

## Bevacizumab (the most-studied partner)
- **Pemetrexed + bevacizumab maintenance** after platinum-based induction recurs across trials as a tolerable, active continuation strategy in nonsquamous NSCLC.
- Randomized/comparative first-line data: pemetrexed/carboplatin (or cisplatin) + bevacizumab vs paclitaxel/carboplatin/bevacizumab (PMID 30900826); carboplatin + pemetrexed + bevacizumab with maintenance bevacizumab (PMID 29848743); cisplatin/pemetrexed + bevacizumab followed by pemetrexed/bevacizumab maintenance (PMID 29644460).
- **Dose and regimen optimization** — different bevacizumab doses with pemetrexed/platinum (PMID 34867332); bevacizumab across platinum doublets (PMID 29171009); with/without bevacizumab as first-line and maintenance (PMID 32406994).
- **Older patients** — bevacizumab + pemetrexed ± carboplatin (65Plus study, PMID 29158696).

## Other antiangiogenics
- Nintedanib, anlotinib, apatinib, ramucirumab, and endostatin appear as VEGF-pathway partners for pemetrexed-based chemotherapy, extending the antiangiogenic rationale beyond bevacizumab.
- Combinations of EGFR-TKI + antiangiogenic + chemotherapy also appear (PMID 36230817), reflecting triplet strategies in EGFR-mutant disease.

## Practical implications
1. **Pemetrexed + bevacizumab maintenance** is the best-established antiangiogenic synergy, prolonging disease control after induction.
2. Benefit is incremental and must be weighed against added toxicity (hypertension, bleeding, proteinuria); patient selection matters.
3. Newer oral antiangiogenics broaden options, especially in regions/settings where bevacizumab access is limited.
