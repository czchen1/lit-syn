# Oncogene-targeted TKIs + pemetrexed — EGFR, ALK/ROS1, MET

In oncogene-driven NSCLC, combining a targeted TKI with pemetrexed-based chemotherapy aims to deepen and prolong responses and suppress resistant subclones. The interaction is **schedule-sensitive**: EGFR-TKIs induce G1 arrest, which can antagonize S-phase-dependent pemetrexed if given fully concurrently, motivating intercalated or combination schedules that have now been tested in Phase III.

## EGFR-TKI + chemotherapy
- **FLAURA2 — osimertinib + pemetrexed/platinum vs osimertinib** in EGFR-mutant advanced NSCLC established that adding chemotherapy to a third-generation EGFR-TKI improves PFS; patient-reported-outcomes analysis is included (PMID 41801128).
- **Aumolertinib + chemotherapy** first-line in EGFR ex19del/L858R NSCLC (PMID 40088185).
- **Intercalated EGFR-TKI + platinum doublet**, randomized Phase III (PMID 40162917); network/first-line comparisons of EGFR-TKI + chemo regimens (PMID 40860888, 35516725, 36215289).
- **EGFR exon 20 insertions** — where classical EGFR-TKIs fail, chemotherapy-based combinations win: **amivantamab + carboplatin/pemetrexed** (PAPILLON-type, PMID 37870976) improved PFS vs chemotherapy; **mobocertinib vs platinum/pemetrexed** (EXCLAIM-2, PMID 39879577) did *not* beat chemotherapy, underscoring the strength of the pemetrexed backbone in this subset.

## ALK / ROS1 / MET
- **Continuing next-generation ALK-TKI with chemotherapy** at progression in ALK+ NSCLC (PMID 41671458) — a beyond-progression combination strategy.
- **Chemo-IO in driver-negative-adjacent settings** — sugemalimab or placebo + chemotherapy (GEMSTONE-302, PMID 37322367).
- Historical benchmarks establishing TKI vs pemetrexed-containing chemotherapy: ceritinib (PMID 30298279) and crizotinib (PMID 25470694) first-line vs chemotherapy in ALK+ NSCLC.
- MET-pathway partners (capmatinib, tepotinib, savolitinib, c-MET) appear as preclinical/early combination candidates with pemetrexed.

## Practical implications
1. **Adding pemetrexed/platinum to an EGFR-TKI (FLAURA2) deepens control** but adds toxicity — selection and sequencing are key.
2. In **EGFR exon 20 insertion** disease, chemotherapy-anchored combinations (amivantamab + carboplatin/pemetrexed) are the validated approach; a pure TKI (mobocertinib) did not outperform the pemetrexed backbone.
3. Full-concurrent EGFR-TKI + pemetrexed risks **G1/S antagonism**; intercalated schedules are the preclinically favored way to combine.
