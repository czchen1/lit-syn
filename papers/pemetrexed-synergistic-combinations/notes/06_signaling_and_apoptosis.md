# Signaling & apoptosis-pathway partners + pemetrexed

Pemetrexed kills by pushing cells past an apoptotic threshold under nucleotide/replication stress. Drugs that lower that threshold — by neutralizing anti-apoptotic proteins, shutting down pro-survival signaling, or destabilizing oncogenic clients — synergize with it. This class is broad and almost entirely preclinical, but several agents have reached early-phase pemetrexed-combination trials.

## Anti-apoptotic (BCL-2 family)
- **BCL-XL is the dominant survival protein in MPM; BH3-mimetic BCL-XL inhibition synergizes with MCL-1 co-targeting and enhances cisplatin killing** in vitro and in vivo (Arulananda et al. 2020, PMID 33298868). A strong rationale for combining BH3-mimetics with the cisplatin/pemetrexed backbone.

## PI3K / AKT / mTOR and transporter-linked exposure
- **Rhein + pemetrexed** synergize via organic-anion-transporter inhibition (raising pemetrexed exposure) and PI3K-AKT-mTOR modulation, increasing apoptosis and autophagy (Bu et al. 2020, PMID 31587272).
- PI3K/AKT/mTOR pathway inhibitors (everolimus, rapamycin analogs, PI3K inhibitors) recur as sensitizers that remove a pro-survival buffer.

## Chaperones, kinases, and degraders
- **HSP90 inhibition (ganetespib) + pemetrexed/platinum** in MPM — MESO-02 Phase Ib, exploiting known antifolate/platinum synergy via destabilization of survival-pathway clients (Fennell et al. 2020, PMID 32669375).
- **SRC inhibition (bosutinib) + pemetrexed** — Phase I in metastatic solid tumors, on the hypothesis that SRC overexpression drives antifolate resistance (Karim et al. 2022, PMID 36547158).
- **PLK1 degradation** — cereblon modulator CC-885 degrades PLK1 and synergizes with volasertib in lung cancer, illustrating targeted-degrader combination strategies where simple PLK1 inhibitor + pemetrexed did not add benefit (Li et al. 2020, PMID 32728610).
- HDAC inhibitors, proteasome inhibitors (bortezomib), STAT3, aurora-kinase, and NAMPT (see DDR note) inhibitors round out the signaling-partner landscape.

## Metabolic-vulnerability partners
- **Arginine deprivation (ADI-PEG20)** potentiates pemetrexed in ASS1-deficient MPM (PMID 34589965; see backbone note).
- One-carbon/folate metabolic nodes (MTHFD2, SHMT2) overlap with the folate note as targetable sensitizers.

## Practical implications
1. **BH3-mimetics (BCL-XL/MCL-1) and HSP90 inhibitors** have the strongest preclinical + early-clinical support in MPM on the pemetrexed backbone.
2. Several partners work partly by **raising pemetrexed exposure or blocking resistance transporters**, not only by cell-intrinsic synergy.
3. Most of this class is still preclinical/Phase I — promising but not yet practice-changing.
