# Chemotherapy backbone — platinum, gemcitabine, taxanes

The reference synergistic combination for pemetrexed is the **platinum doublet**. Pemetrexed depletes dNTP pools (blocking DNA synthesis and repair) while platinum forms DNA adducts — a classic "starve the repair, then damage the DNA" synergy that underpins the standard first-line regimen in nonsquamous NSCLC and the only standard doublet in malignant pleural mesothelioma (MPM).

## Platinum (cisplatin / carboplatin)
- Cisplatin + pemetrexed is the established MPM backbone and the nonsquamous-NSCLC standard; most novel combinations in this collection are built *on top of* it.
- **Schedule / sequence dependence** is a repeated finding: pre-depleting nucleotides with pemetrexed sensitizes to subsequent DNA damage. See radiosensitization (`08`) for the clearest schedule data.
- **Resistance reversal on the platinum backbone** is a major theme in MPM:
  - **Hypoxia lowers PCFT and blunts pemetrexed** (Li Petri et al. 2020, PMID 32493992); LDH-A inhibition is proposed to counter the hypoxic-glycolytic escape.
  - **CDA induction sensitizes to sequential capecitabine** — standard chemo raises cytidine deaminase, priming MPM cells for capecitabine/5-FU (Karatkevich et al. 2024, PMID 39107509).
  - **Arginine deprivation potentiates pemetrexed** in ASS1-deficient MPM: pegargiminase (ADI-PEG20) + pemetrexed/cisplatin, Phase 1 expansion (Szlosarek et al. 2020, PMID 34589965).

## Gemcitabine
- Gemcitabine + pemetrexed and gemcitabine-containing schedules recur as antifolate/antimetabolite pairings; because both act in S phase, their interaction is strongly schedule-dependent (see the CDK4/6i and DDR notes for the antagonism-vs-synergy discussion).

## Taxanes
- Taxane + pemetrexed/platinum backbones appear mainly in the NSCLC combination-trial literature; taxanes contribute G2/M arrest complementary to pemetrexed's S-phase block.

## Practical implications
1. The platinum doublet is the validated synergistic core; the productive research question is **what to add or how to sequence**, not whether to combine.
2. **Transporter and metabolic context (PCFT, hypoxia, ASS1, CDA)** modulate the doublet's efficacy and offer rational sensitizing strategies.
3. Mesothelioma is the most active testbed for backbone-augmenting combinations because of universal resistance to cisplatin/pemetrexed.
