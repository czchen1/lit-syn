# Spleen and immune-cell targeting

## Why spleen matters

The spleen is the largest secondary lymphoid organ and the primary site where:
- Antigen-presenting cells (DCs, macrophages) prime T and B cell responses.
- Marginal-zone B cells and splenic DCs encounter blood-borne antigens.
- Tolerance can be induced via mRNA-encoded regulatory factors.

Spleen-targeted LNPs are thus critical for:
1. **mRNA cancer immunotherapy** — encoding tumor antigens, cytokines, or checkpoint modulators for immune activation.
2. **Autoimmune tolerance** — delivering regulatory mRNA (IL-10, TGF-β, modified autoantigens) to tolerogenic DCs.
3. **In vivo CAR generation** — transfecting T or NK cells in the spleen with CAR mRNA.

## Formulation strategies for spleen targeting

### Anionic SORT
- Adding 10–30 mol% anionic lipid (e.g., 18PA, DOPS) to standard LNPs shifts tropism from liver → spleen (Cheng et al. 2020).
- Mechanism: altered corona recruits proteins that direct uptake by splenic macrophages and marginal-zone B cells.

### Ionizable-lipid headgroup tuning
- **Higher apparent pKa (6.5–7.0)** correlates with spleen selectivity.
- **Imidazole headgroups**: Zn(II)-adjuvanted imidazole ILs provide spleen-targeted immune activation (Han et al. 2026).
- **Cyclic tertiary amines**: Ugi-reaction-derived lipids with cyclic amine heads enable spleen-selective delivery (Wang et al. 2025).

### Zwitterionic polymer lipids
- Li et al. (2025, PMID 40389003): zwitterionic polymer-lipid hybrid NPs enable selective organ targeting, including spleen, via surface charge modulation.

### Endocytosis-dependent selectivity
- Shi et al. (2026): identified ionizable lipids whose spleen expression depends on specific endocytic pathways (clathrin vs caveolae) — matching the endocytic machinery of target splenocytes.

## Immune-cell-specific targeting

Beyond organ-level spleen targeting, several papers address **cell-type-specific** immune-cell delivery:

### Dendritic cells
- Chen et al. (2026): H-type ionizable lipid LNPs targeting splenic dendritic cells for mRNA vaccine transfection; structure optimized for DC endocytic machinery.
- Mannose-conjugated PEG-lipids direct uptake via mannose receptor (CD206) on DCs.

### Macrophages
- Shi et al. (2026) PMID 41381536: calixsalan-based ionizable lipid for macrophage-targeted Trem2 siRNA delivery (atherosclerosis context, but LNP engineering insight applies to cancer).
- Khan et al. (2026): endosome-escaping macrophage-targeted NP platform for miR-146a delivery.

### T cells
- Anti-CD5 antibody-conjugated LNPs: Rurik et al. (Nature 2022, in companion collection) established the paradigm; several 2025–2026 papers optimize the antibody–LNP conjugation chemistry.
- CD4/CD8 bispecific targeting under development.

### Hematopoietic stem cells (HSCs)
- Anti-CD117 antibody-LNP conjugates for HSC-targeted gene editing (bone marrow); several papers in this corpus address the LNP engineering challenge.
