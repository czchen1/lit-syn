# Brain and CNS delivery

## The blood–brain barrier challenge

The BBB is the most restrictive biological barrier for LNP delivery:
- Tight junctions between brain endothelial cells prevent paracellular transport.
- Efflux pumps (P-gp, BCRP) actively export substrates.
- Astrocyte endfeet form a second barrier.
- Only lipophilic molecules < 400 Da cross freely; LNPs (60–150 nm) cannot.

## Strategies in this corpus

### Ionizable-lipid-intrinsic BBB crossing
- **Berberine-inspired ionizable lipids** (Bian et al. 2025): berberine's planar aromatic headgroup promotes self-structure stabilization and brain tropism. In vivo bioluminescence shows brain expression ~5× over standard MC3-based LNPs.
- **Dual-tail polyamine lipids** with optimized log P: brain-penetrating lipids identified by balancing hydrophobicity (high enough for membrane interaction) with water solubility (low enough pKa for endosomal release in neurons).

### Receptor-mediated transcytosis
- **Transferrin receptor (TfR)**: anti-TfR antibody or peptide conjugated to PEG-lipid enables receptor-mediated transcytosis across brain endothelium. Multiple papers validate this approach, though concerns about TfR saturation and lysosomal routing persist.
- **ApoE/LDLR pathway**: certain LNP coronas (especially those enriched in ApoE) can exploit the LDLR on brain endothelium. This is serendipitous — most ApoE-rich coronas target hepatocytes, but specific formulations show brain preference.
- **Glucose transporter (GLUT1)**: glucose-conjugated LNPs exploit GLUT1 transcytosis; early-stage, small dataset.

### AI-validated brain targeting
- Sela et al. (2025, PMID 40957853): AI model trained on ~200 lipid–brain-delivery pairs predicts BBB-crossing formulations; 3/5 top predictions validated in vivo with significant brain expression.

### Route-based approaches
- **Intrathecal injection**: bypasses BBB entirely; papers focus on LNP stability in CSF and neuronal tropism after intrathecal delivery.
- **Intranasal delivery**: exploits olfactory/trigeminal nerve pathways to bypass BBB; LNPs must penetrate nasal mucus and be stable in nasal mucosa environment.
- **Focused ultrasound (FUS) + microbubbles**: transient BBB opening allows IV-administered LNPs to enter brain parenchyma; several papers combine FUS with brain-optimized LNP formulations.

## Neuron vs glia selectivity

Even after crossing the BBB, cell-type selectivity within the brain is important:
- Neuronal tropism: requires different lipid composition than astrocyte or microglia transfection.
- Sela et al. (2025) report neuronal tropism validation via NeuN co-staining with LNP-delivered reporter.

## Current limitations

1. **Low absolute delivery**: even the best brain-targeting LNPs deliver <1% of injected dose to brain (vs 30–80% to liver for standard LNPs).
2. **Inflammation risk**: BBB disruption (FUS) or high-dose intrathecal injection can cause neuroinflammation.
3. **Payload persistence**: mRNA expression in neurons is transient (~24–72 h); gene editing may be needed for durable effects.
