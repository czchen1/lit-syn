# Lung-targeted LNP delivery

Lung targeting is the most advanced extrahepatic application, pursued via two complementary routes: **systemic IV formulation** (SORT, charge tuning) and **inhalation/nebulization** (direct pulmonary deposition).

## Systemic (IV) lung targeting

### SORT mechanism
Adding 20–50 mol% cationic SORT lipid (DOTAP, DDAB) to a standard four-component LNP redirects expression from liver to lung, primarily via:
- Protein corona enrichment with complement C3, vitronectin, and IgM → lung endothelial uptake.
- Increased positive surface charge at physiological pH → electrostatic interaction with lung endothelium.

### Ionizable-lipid-intrinsic lung targeting
Several 2025–2026 studies demonstrate lung selectivity without SORT additives:
- **Mass spectrometry-guided HTS** (Liu et al. 2026): lipid-MS profiling identifies structural features (e.g., symmetric tails, piperazine heads) that predict lung transfection.
- **Charge-steric balance** (He et al. 2025): defined tail branching + headgroup pKa in a narrow window (6.4–6.7) yields lung selectivity.
- **AMP-derived lipids** (Somu Naidu et al. 2025): antimicrobial peptide-inspired ionizable lipids with optimized alkyl tails transfect lung endothelial cells.

### Surface-hydrophobicity tuning
Papers from 2025–2026 show that surface hydrophobicity (measured by contact angle, pyrene fluorescence, or HLB calculation) correlates with lung vs liver tropism — more hydrophobic surfaces favor lung.

## Inhalation / nebulization

### Challenges
Aerosolizing LNPs introduces unique stresses:
1. **Shear and cavitation** during nebulization disrupt particle integrity.
2. **Mucus barrier**: 1–10 µm mucus layer traps particles > 200 nm.
3. **Pulmonary surfactant** (dipalmitoylphosphatidylcholine, SP-A/SP-D) adsorbs onto LNPs and may inhibit cell uptake.
4. **Macrophage clearance**: alveolar macrophages engulf deposited particles.

### Formulation solutions
- **PEG-lipid optimization**: higher PEG-lipid mol% (3–5% vs standard 1.5%) improves mucus penetration and nebulization stability.
- **N-acetylcysteine (NAC) surface remodeling** (Cheng et al. 2026): mucolytic agent on LNP surface disrupts mucus and enhances post-deposition transport.
- **Vibrating mesh nebulizers** (Aerogen Solo, eFlow) preserve LNP integrity better than jet nebulizers.
- **Freeze-drying + cryomilling** (Jansen et al. 2026): dry-powder inhaler formulations of sa-mRNA LNPs maintain potency after room-temperature storage.
- **Chitosan coating**: mucoadhesive polymer shell improves retention and reduces clearance.

### Applications
- Gene therapy for cystic fibrosis (CFTR mRNA).
- Pulmonary fibrosis (STING editing in alveolar macrophages: Wang et al. 2026).
- Lung cancer (in situ cytokine/CAR expression).
- Inhaled mRNA vaccines (tuberculosis, influenza — excluded from this collection but referenced in engineering context).
