# Effector cell sources for GD2 CAR products

## 1. Bulk autologous αβ-T cells from PBMC

The default for most clinical GD2 CAR-T products. PBMC is collected by leukapheresis, T cells are activated with anti-CD3/CD28 (Dynabeads, TransAct, or plate-bound OKT3 + anti-CD28), transduced with retrovirus or lentivirus on day 3–4, and expanded for 7–14 days. Examples:

- Long 2015 (PMID 25939063) — preclinical exhaustion model, MSGV-14g2a-28z and 14g2a-BBz.
- Mount 2018 (PMID 29662203) — MSCV 14g2a-CD8TM-4-1BB-CD3ζ; activation: anti-CD3/CD28 beads at 3:1 bead:cell, 40 IU/mL IL-2 for 3 days, RetroNectin on day 3–4, 300 IU/mL IL-2 during expansion.
- Heczey 2017 (PMID 28602436) clinical GD2-CAR3 — PBMC → OKT3+CD28+IL-2 or IL-7/IL-15 → RetroNectin transduction day 3 with SFG.iC9.GD2-CAR3 → expansion → patient infusion.
- Quintarelli 2018 (PMID 29872565) — same Brenner-lineage SFG vectors compared 2G vs 3G, IL-2 vs IL-7+IL-15.
- Del Bufalo 2023 NEJM GD2-CART01 (clinical follow-on of Quintarelli 2018 chemistry).
- Majzner 2022 / Monje 2025 (PMID 35130560, 39537919) — CliniMACS Prodigy-manufactured GD2-4-1BB-CD3ζ + iCasp9 retroviral CAR.
- Locatelli 2025 (PMID 40926103) — phase 1/2 NB trial with the Bambino Gesù GD2-CART01.
- Li 2025 — long-term follow-up of GD2-CART01 cohort.

## 2. CD4 + CD8 immunomagnetically selected products

To remove monocytes, B cells, and erythrocytes, and to control the CD4:CD8 ratio in the final product. Most modern closed-system workflows use this.

- Majzner 2022 (PMID 35130560 Methods, lines 1206–1213): "Autologous peripheral blood mononuclear cells (PBMCs) were collected via apheresis and GD2-CAR T cells were manufactured using the closed-system CliniMACS Prodigy (Miltenyi). In brief, CD4 and CD8 T cells were selected and transduced with one bicistronic retroviral vector encoding an iCasp9 domain… and a GD2-4-1BB-CD3ζ CAR containing the 14g2a scFv under control of an MSCV promoter."
- Glienke 2022 (PMID 35401506 Methods, lines 156–170): "CliniMACS® CD4 Reagent, CliniMACS® CD8 Reagent and CliniMACS® PBS/EDTA buffer" used to enrich CD4+ and CD8+ T cells on the Prodigy from a 12-day apheresis-to-formulated-product workflow.
- Bambino Gesù GD2-CART01 (Quintarelli 2018 PMID 29872565) — PBMC isolated from buffy coats by density-gradient (Eurobio), then activated with immobilized OKT3 + anti-CD28. The clinical product enriches CD3+ T cells but does not pre-select CD4 vs CD8.

## 3. Naive- / stem-cell-memory-enriched products

- Cappabianca 2024 (PMID 38699288): "metabolic priming" protocol uses Immunocult XF medium + IL-2 during the activation/edit window, then IL-7/IL-15 in the expansion window, generating MP TRAC-CAR T cells highly enriched for CD45RA+CD62L+CCR7+ TSCM phenotype.
- Mueller 2022 (PMID 36382633) — TRAC-targeted virus-free CRISPR CAR with a similar bias toward less-differentiated memory subsets.
- Gargett 2024 (PMID 38754916) melanoma trial — manufactured cells skew away from CD45RA−CCR7− effector memory toward CD45RA+CCR7+ naive-like / CD45RA+CCR7− TEMRA mix.
- Dasatinib-priming protocols (Majzner 2022 PMID 35130560; Monje 2025 PMID 39537919) — addition of the BCR-ABL/TKI dasatinib on days 3 and 5 of culture transiently halts TCR-proximal kinase signaling and preserves memory/stem-cell phenotype.

## 4. Virus-specific T cells (VSTs / CTLs)

The Brenner group's approach to provide a "second TCR signal" by transducing CAR into already-primed EBV/CMV/AdV-specific cytotoxic T cells, whose persistence is much longer than activated bulk T cells:

- Pule 2008 (PMID 18978797): patient-derived EBV-specific CTLs were generated by stimulating PBMCs with autologous LCLs, then transduced with the GD2-Zeta retroviral cassette. The Zeta-5 / Zeta-6 PCR tags allowed simultaneous infusion of CAR-CTLs (Zeta-5) and CAR-ATCs (Zeta-6) and per-construct quantitation in vivo by PCR-amplicon cutting. CAR-CTL persistence was ~24-fold longer than CAR-ATC in the same patient.
- Louis 2011 (PMID 21984804): updated follow-up; CAR-CTLs persisted >2 yr in some patients.
- Caruana 2015 (PMID 25691731) — transient CAR-VST manufacturing protocols.
- Tanaka 2017 — CMV-specific GD2 CAR-T variant.

## 5. Vα24-invariant NKT cells (CD1d-restricted)

- Heczey 2014 (PMID 25049283): first GD2-CAR-NKT preclinical study. NKT cells from PBMC are stimulated with αGalCer-pulsed autologous irradiated PBMCs and IL-2, transduced with retroviral vectors carrying CAR.GD2 on day 3, and expanded with rIL-2. Expansion rate is 5,700-fold (range 842 to 14,300-fold; n=5). The 4-1BB-containing CAR drives Th1 polarization; CD28+4-1BB combination gives the best in-vivo persistence. CD3z-only CAR (Gz) is functional but Th2-leaning.
- Xu 2019 — co-expression of GD2 CAR and IL-15 in NKTs to drive persistence.
- Heczey 2020 (Nat Med, PMID 33046868 supplement): clinical GD2-CAR.15 NKT product manufactured at Texas Children's CAGT cGMP facility. Per Supp Table 2: starting apheresis NKT purity ~8–54%, expanded for 9–15 days, final product 90–97% NKT purity, 20–70% CAR+, 1,500–4,200-fold expansion. CAR-NKT products are predominantly CD4-positive in patients 1 and 2, more CD4-negative in patient 3.
- Heczey 2023 (Nat Med, PMID 37188782 supplement): updated GINAKIT trial. Manufacturing parameters: "NKTs were selected with iNKT-specific microbeads from peripheral blood apheresis products, stimulated with alpha-galactosylceramide-loaded, irradiated peripheral blood mononuclear cells, and transduced with a gamma-retroviral vector encoding the GD2-CAR and IL15 transgenes." (Supp Fig 1 legend, lines 109–116). Final products averaged >80% NKT purity, with ~40% CAR-expression.
- Tian 2025 (PMID 40044579, 39800376): correspondence and follow-up describing hyperleukocytosis observed at the highest dose level (DL5, 1×10⁸ CAR-NKT/m²) of GD2-CAR.15 NKTs, attributed in part to use of K562-based αGalCer-loaded aAPCs for secondary stimulation instead of PBMCs.

## 6. γδ T cells (Vγ9Vδ2 and Vδ1)

- Capsomidis 2018 (PMID 29310916): GD2 CAR-γδT cells. PBMC γδT subsets are activated and expanded with one of: (1) anti-CD3/anti-CD28 + IL-2 (preferentially expands αβ T cells but also Vδ1), (2) zoledronate (ZOL) + IL-2 (preferentially expands Vγ9Vδ2), (3) concanavalin A + IL-2/IL-4 (preferentially expands Vδ1). Each subset is then transduced with the GD2-CD28-CD3ζ CAR by gamma-retroviral spinoculation 48 h after activation. Both Vδ1 (ConA+) and Vδ2 (ZOL+) γδ CAR-T cells kill GD2+ neuroblastoma cells; Vδ2 CAR cells can also cross-present soluble antigens (e.g. MART1) to bystander αβ T cells, suggesting in-vivo vaccinal effects.
- Caforio 2021 (PMID 33737337): the GD2-CART01 product (CD28-4-1BB-CD3ζ + iC9) is also tested in MB-cocultures with γδT subsets.

## 7. NK cells

### 7.1 NK-92 clinical cell line

- Bodden 2023 (PMID 37686586): "Co-Expression of an IL-15 Superagonist Facilitates Self-Enrichment of GD2-Targeted CAR-NK Cells." NK-92 cells transduced with a lentiviral cassette encoding a hu14.18-CD28-CD3ζ CAR alone (hu14.18.28.z) or together with RD-IL15 (an IL-15 super-agonist fusion linked via P2A). Construct details (Methods, lines 151–162): hu14.18 VH-(G4S)3-VL scFv → Myc-tag → modified CD8α hinge → CD28 TM → CD3ζ ICD; codon-optimized cassette in lentiviral transfer plasmid pSIEW (pHR'SIN-cPPT-SIEW), VSV-G pseudotyped. The IL-15 superagonist enables IL-2-independent proliferation of the NK product.

### 7.2 Primary expanded NK cells

- Esser 2012 (PMID 21595822) — early GD2 CAR transduction of healthy-donor NK cells.
- Antonucci 2022 (PMID 35603195) — GD2 CAR in K562-expanded primary NK cells.
- Chiavelli 2024 (PMID 38302615) — GD2 CAR-NK using membrane-bound IL-15 + 4-1BBL aAPC-expanded primary NK cells.

### 7.3 iPSC-derived NK

Several 2024–2026 collection papers describe GD2 CAR knock-in to TRAC or AAVS1 locus of induced pluripotent stem cells, differentiated to NK cells with adapter cassettes (CD16, IL-15, mbIL-21).

## 8. Cytokine-induced killer (CIK) cells

- Prapa 2015 (PMID 26298772): a GD2 CAR (IgM-derived scFv → CD8 hinge-TM → 4-1BB → CD3ζ) is expressed in CIK cells via retroviral transduction on RetroNectin-coated plates. CIK cells (CD3+CD56+) provide a mixed innate/adaptive killing platform suited to GD2-low targets.

## 9. iPSC-derived T cells

Recent (2024–2026) papers in the collection describe TRAC knock-in of GD2 CAR into iPSC-derived T cell intermediates, with the goal of an off-the-shelf, expandable product without donor-to-donor variability.

## 10. Allogeneic / donor-derived products

- Quintarelli 2025 (Nat Med, PMID 39815015 supplement): hospital-exemption protocol for **ALLO_GD2-CART01**, an allogeneic version of GD2-CART01 expanded from intra-family HLA-compatible donor mononuclear cells. The iC9 safety switch is the principal GvHD-mitigation mechanism. Dosing rationale: up to 10×10⁶ CAR+ cells/kg, with the Brudno cohort precedent of 7.8×10⁶ allogeneic CD19-CAR+/kg cited as a safety benchmark. Lymphodepletion is fludarabine + cyclophosphamide as in the autologous protocol.
- Locatelli 2025 (PMID 40926103) — also discusses use of donor-derived cells in patients without an autologous option.

## 11. Macrophage- and microglia-based GD2 CAR products

A handful of 2024–2026 preclinical papers in the collection generate **CAR-M** (CAR-macrophages, often via iPSC differentiation) and **CAR-microglia** carrying GD2 binders to leverage phagocytosis and antigen-cross-presentation. Most use lentiviral or PiggyBac delivery of an anti-GD2 scFv fused to MERTK-, FcγRI-, or CD3ζ-based intracellular signaling tails.

## 12. MSCs as carriers

- Chulanetra and others in collection — GD2 scFv-armored mesenchymal stromal cells used to deliver TRAIL or IFN-γ to GD2+ tumors. Not strictly CAR-T but included as a non-T effector platform for GD2 targeting.
