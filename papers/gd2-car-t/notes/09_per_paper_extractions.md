### Majzner RG, Ramakrishna S, Yeom KW et al. (2022) — Nature

**PMID** 35130560 · **Title**: *GD2-CAR T cell therapy for H3K27M-mutated diffuse midline gliomas.* · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35130560/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-022-04489-4)

**Majzner, Ramakrishna, Yeom et al. (Nature 2022; Stanford).** First-in-human phase I of GD2 CAR-T in 4 patients with H3K27M+ DIPG/sDMG. Construct: 14g2a scFv + CD8α-TM + 4-1BB + CD3ζ + upstream iCasp9 (from Bellicum Pharmaceuticals), expressed from a single bicistronic retroviral vector under the MSCV promoter. Manufacturing: closed-system CliniMACS Prodigy (Miltenyi). Apheresis-derived PBMCs → CD4 and CD8 immunomagnetic selection → activation, transduction, and expansion for **7 days** in IL-7 + IL-15 (12.5 ng/mL each, Miltenyi) + tyrosine-kinase inhibitor **dasatinib** on days 3 and 5 to suppress tonic signaling and preserve fitness. Doses: 1×10⁶ CAR-T/kg IV (DL1) and 3×10⁶ CAR-T/kg IV (DL2) with Cy/Flu LD (Cy 500 mg/m²/day, Flu 25 mg/m²/day d-4 to d-2). Patients with clinical benefit eligible for repeat ICV infusions via Ommaya without LD (10–30×10⁶ cells, 3 mL). Protocol explicitly engineered around TIAN management: routine and symptom-prompted ICP monitoring, mandatory Ommaya in DIPG, prophylactic tocilizumab + anakinra + corticosteroids, iCasp9 as rescue. Three of four patients had radiographic and clinical responses.

### Heczey A, Courtney AN, Montalbano A et al. (2020) — Nature medicine

**PMID** 33046868 · **Title**: *Anti-GD2 CAR-NKT cells in patients with relapsed or refractory neuroblastoma: an interim analysis.* · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33046868/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-020-1074-2)
