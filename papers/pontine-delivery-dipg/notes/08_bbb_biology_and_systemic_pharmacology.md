# Pontine BBB biology, efflux, and systemic BBB-penetrant pharmacology

Every route decision rests on an empirical question: how much of the barrier is actually
intact in a given DIPG, and what does it do to the specific molecule?

## What is known about the barrier in DIPG/DMG

- **Structure.** El-Khouly 2021 (PMID 37284626) examined the neurovascular unit in DIPG
  biopsy and autopsy tissue against age-matched pons; Haumann 2020 (PATH-04 abstract)
  compared end-stage DMG pons to healthy controls, framing the "intact BBB" claim in
  tissue terms rather than by imaging inference alone.
- **Regulation.** Wei 2021 (HGG-22 abstract) reported differences in BBB function and
  increased Angpt1 in H3K27M DIPG models, i.e. the tumour may actively maintain barrier
  integrity — a delivery-relevant mechanism, not an incidental property.
- **Heterogeneity.** Contrast enhancement in DMG is variable and changes after
  radiotherapy (Rameh 2025, PMID 40280708), so a single
  patient's barrier state is a moving target across the treatment course.

## Efflux transporters set the ceiling for systemic dosing

- ABCG2/ABCB1 promote brainstem gliomagenesis and blunt dasatinib efficacy in DIPG models
  (2014 abstract; Tsvankin 2020, PMID 31225627 for the therapeutic corollary).
- Power's CED/efflux work (HGG-13 2021 and DDEL-16 2020 abstracts; Power 2023,
  PMID 37128506) shows brain distribution and clearance of alisertib depend jointly on
  P-gp/BCRP and on the delivery method — the two variables are not separable.
- Practical consequence: an agent's in vitro potency in H3K27M cells predicts little; the
  relevant quantities are unbound brain concentration, Kp,uu, and residence time.

## Pharmacokinetic evidence generated for pontine trials

- Panobinostat: PBTC phase 1 in children with DIPG (Monje 2023, PMID 37526549), CNS
  distribution modelling to guide pediatric dosing (Zhang 2023, PMID 37827699), non-human
  primate blood-versus-CSF levels (EPCT-09 2021 abstract), and a ¹⁸F-radiolabelled analogue
  enabling PET-guided delivery assessment (Kommidi 2018, PMID 29456798). This is the most
  complete example in the field of pairing a drug with delivery measurements.
- ONC201/ONC206 (imipridones) are used precisely because they are BBB-penetrant; the
  collection includes resistance mechanisms (Jackson 2020 abstract, AKT-driven resistance)
  and combination work with locoregional CAR-T (Timpanaro 2026, PMID 41639009).
- Radiosensitiser and metabolic programmes assume systemic dosing plus radiotherapy
  (Shen 2025, PMID 39575457, on mitochondrial bioenergetics/hypoxia; Liu 2024,
  PMID 38644565, anlotinib with RT/TMZ in H3K27M-DMG).

## Interpretation

Systemic pharmacology and physical delivery are complements, not competitors. The
literature supports a simple decision rule: if a molecule cannot be engineered to a
usable unbound brain concentration at tolerable systemic exposure, it needs a route
(CED, FUS, CSF); if it can, the route becomes a way to *raise the local ceiling* or to
shorten the exposure needed. In either case, unmeasured delivery is the main reason
DIPG trials cannot distinguish drug failure from delivery failure.
