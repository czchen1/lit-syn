# Radiation: the one modality that always reaches the pons

Radiotherapy is the only treatment that reliably deposits its "dose" in a DIPG, so it
appears in this collection as the comparator and partner for every other route.

## Baseline and re-treatment

- Conventional focal RT remains standard; hypofractionated schedules are non-inferior in
  meta-analysis (Viani 2024, PMID 39144263), which matters for delivery because it changes
  how often a child is available for procedures.
- Re-irradiation is widely practised at progression (Mankuzhy 2024, PMID 38430472;
  Nuryadi 2025, PMID 41148593 systematic review/meta-analysis; the Spanish and other
  multi-centre abstracts), and proton re-irradiation near the brainstem defines the
  tolerance envelope any combined-delivery protocol must live inside (Hug 2026,
  PMID 41488407).
- Craniospinal irradiation appears as rescue for disseminated disease (DIPG-04 2024
  abstract) — the radiation analogue of choosing a CSF route over a focal one.

## Radiation used to enable or amplify drug delivery

- **Sequencing with BBB opening.** Tazhibi 2024 (PMID 38555449) shows FUS-BBBO is safe and
  feasible with moderately hypofractionated RT in brainstem DMG — the key demonstration
  that a physical delivery procedure can be interleaved with the radiation backbone.
- **Radiosensitiser delivery by CED.** Yang 2011 (PMID 20577779) combined CED carboplatin
  with radiotherapy in an experimental brainstem/glioma model, and DIPG-41 (2024 abstract)
  reports CED-mediated radiosensitisation for DMG; MTX110 and carboplatin/valproate CED
  programmes are also designed around the post-RT window.
- **Radiation as the payload.** CED of radio-immunotheranostic ¹²⁴I-omburtamab
  (Souweidane 2018, PMID 29914796; full dose-escalation report Souweidane 2025,
  PMID 39969230, with erratum PMID 40489365) delivers the isotope directly, making
  dosimetry a delivery measurement. Rhenium-186 liposomes function as
  convection-delivered brachytherapy (Phillips 2012, PMID 22427110), and permanent
  implants have measurable dosimetry (GammaTile, Zhang 2024, PMID 38902561).
- **Boron neutron capture therapy** is a delivery problem disguised as radiotherapy: the
  boron carrier must accumulate in tumour. Salvage BNCT has been given to children with
  recurrent DMG (Huang 2023, PMID 36821007) and compassionately for brainstem tumours
  (Chen 2022, PMID 35455057; Liao 2025, PMID 40103665 for clival chordoma with brainstem
  involvement), and carrier engineering continues (Chen 2026, PMID 41738936, ¹²⁴I-labelled
  exosome BNCT platform; Miyata 2011, PMID 21273928, transferrin-targeted boron/iodine
  liposomes).

## Interactions to respect

- Radiation changes the barrier and the tumour vasculature, so contrast enhancement and
  presumed BBB state after RT are not the same as at diagnosis (Rameh 2025,
  PMID 40280708).
- Oedema tolerance is the shared limiting resource: FUS-BBBO, CED infusate volume and
  re-irradiation all draw on it, so combined protocols must budget it explicitly.
- Devices and implants must be radiotherapy-planning compatible; catheter and reservoir
  hardware affects dose calculation and cannot be ignored in the plan.
