# Clinical experience with γδ T cell therapy

γδ T cell therapy has one of the longest clinical track records of any "innate‑like" cell therapy — and, until recently, one of the most underwhelming. The arc runs from autologous Vγ9Vδ2 (modest, transient responses) to allogeneic engineered products and agonist biologics (the current frontier).

## Generation 1 — autologous / in‑vivo Vγ9Vδ2 (≈2003–2015)

Two formats were tested:
1. **In‑vivo activation:** systemic **aminobisphosphonate (zoledronate/pamidronate) + low‑dose IL‑2** to expand endogenous Vγ9Vδ2 cells (e.g. PMID 20491785).
2. **Adoptive transfer:** ex‑vivo zoledronate/BrHPP + IL‑2‑expanded autologous Vγ9Vδ2 cells reinfused.

Indications spanned **lymphoma/myeloma** (Wilhelm 2003), **prostate** (Dieli 2007), **renal**, **breast**, **lung** (zoledronate‑activated γδ products, e.g. PMID, "Experience of Autologous Immunotherapy for NSCLC Using Zoledronate‑Activated Gammadelta T Cells"), **colorectal**, and **HCC**.

**Verdict:** consistently **safe** (low‑grade, infusion/cytokine‑related toxicity; little GvHD even in allogeneic pilots), with **detectable immune/PD responses** but **low objective response rates** and short‑lived persistence. Failure modes blamed on: scarce/exhausted patient Vγ9Vδ2 pools, repeated‑stimulation **senescence**, IL‑2 toxicity, poor tumor trafficking, and the immunosuppressive TME.

## Generation 2 — allogeneic, off‑the‑shelf, and engineered (≈2018–present)

The field pivoted to **banked allogeneic** and **engineered** products, exploiting HLA‑independence (low GvHD):

- **ADI‑001** (Adicet) — **allogeneic anti‑CD20 CAR Vδ1** cells; preclinical basis PMID 35136603; clinical testing in **r/r B‑NHL** (GLEAN), with reported responses including CRs, validating the off‑the‑shelf CAR‑Vδ1 concept; subsequently extended toward autoimmune indications.
- **DOT cells** (Delta One T, Vδ1) — clinical‑grade allogeneic Vδ1 platform (GammaDelta Therapeutics / Takeda lineage), AML and solid‑tumor oriented.
- **GDX012 / GDA‑201 / agen products and total‑γδ allogeneic** programs in AML and solid tumors.
- **CAR‑γδ in solid/CNS tumors:** **intrathecal allogeneic B7‑H3 CAR‑γδ** for leptomeningeal metastasis (recent first‑in‑human, see index); **γδ‑enriched CAR‑T** for bone‑metastatic CRPC (PMID 37134157); intracranial MGMT‑modified γδ + temozolomide for high‑grade glioma (PMID, "Adoptive cell therapy for high grade gliomas…").
- **TEG001** — αβ T cells engineered with a defined high‑affinity Vγ9Vδ2 TCR; first‑in‑human in hematologic malignancies.
- **Haploidentical γδ cells** — complete remissions in chemorefractory B‑NHL (PMID, "Haploidentical γδ T Cells Induce Complete Remission…").

## Generation 2.5 — agonists and engagers (drug, not cell)

- **ICT01 (anti‑BTN3A agonist, Imcheck)** — activates endogenous Vγ9Vδ2 cells; the **EVICTION** trial tested it ± anti‑PD‑1 in solid and hematologic tumors.
- **γδ bispecific engagers** (e.g. CD1d×Vδ2 LAVA platform; tumor‑antigen×Vγ9) — off‑the‑shelf biologics recruiting endogenous γδ cells.
- **rIL‑15 / IL‑15 agonists** to support γδ (and NK) cells in vivo (PMID, 2025 phase‑1 rIL‑15 pharmacodynamics study).

## What the clinical record teaches
1. **Safety is not the bottleneck** — γδ products (autologous and allogeneic) are reproducibly well tolerated, including limited GvHD, which is the central enabling fact for off‑the‑shelf development.
2. **Efficacy bottleneck = persistence, trafficking, and the TME**, not specificity.
3. **Hematologic > solid** so far: the clearest responses (CAR‑Vδ1 anti‑CD20, haploidentical γδ, AbTCR/γδ‑TCR‑T CD19) are in **B‑cell malignancies and AML**; solid‑tumor efficacy remains the hard problem driving the Vδ1/armoring/combination strategies.
4. **The modern bet** is allogeneic + engineered + combination, with subset choice (Vδ1 for solid tumors) and armoring (IL‑15, checkpoint resistance) as the main levers.
