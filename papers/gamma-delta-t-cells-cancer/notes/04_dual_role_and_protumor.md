# The double‑edged sword: protumor γδ T cells

The single most important nuance in this literature is that γδ T cells are **not uniformly antitumor**. The same lineage that supplies potent cytotoxic effectors also supplies one of the most reliably **protumor** lymphocyte populations described in cancer biology. Any honest synthesis — and any therapeutic program — must account for both. Recent reviews capture the framing directly: "Gamma Delta T Cells in the Tumour Microenvironment: A Double‑Edged Sword" (PMID 41086872) and the dual‑role breast‑cancer review (PMID 41161891).

## The protumor program: IL‑17⁺ γδ T17 cells

The principal villain is the **IL‑17‑producing γδ cell (γδ T17)**, typically RORγt⁺/CCR6⁺ and largely TCR‑Vγ‑restricted in mice. Mechanisms by which γδ T17 cells promote cancer:

- **Myeloid recruitment / immunosuppression.** IL‑17 drives expansion and mobilization of **neutrophils and MDSCs**, which suppress CD8⁺ T cells and create a pro‑metastatic niche. The landmark causal demonstration is Coffelt et al. (*Nature* 2015): IL‑17⁺ γδ cells → G‑CSF/neutrophils → suppression of cytotoxic T cells → pulmonary/lymph‑node metastasis in a breast cancer model.
- **Angiogenesis and proliferation.** IL‑17 (with IL‑22) promotes tumor angiogenesis and epithelial proliferation, prominent in **colorectal** and **hepatocellular** cancers.
- **Radioresistance.** IL‑17‑producing γδ cells in the TME promote **radioresistance** (PMID 41055972) — a direct therapeutic liability when combining radiation with immunotherapy.

## Other suppressive / protumor γδ states
- **Regulatory γδ cells** producing IL‑10/TGF‑β or expressing inhibitory ligands.
- **Exhausted γδ cells** driven by chronic TME signals; in cervical cancer, **BTN3A1** on tumor cells was shown to *drive Vγ9Vδ2 exhaustion* rather than activation (see index), inverting the usual logic.
- **Senescent/anergic** phenotypes after repeated phosphoantigen stimulation.

## The prognosis literature — and why it is contradictory
- The optimistic anchor: Gentles et al. (*Nat Med* 2015) found a γδ signature among the most favorable prognostic immune populations pan‑cancer.
- The correction: that deconvolution signature was later argued to be **confounded** (it partly captured other cytotoxic cells), and direct studies show **context dependence** — γδ infiltration is favorable in some tumors (often Vδ1/IFN‑γ‑biased) and unfavorable in others (often γδ17‑biased, e.g. some colorectal, breast, HCC, pancreatic settings).
- Practical reading: **subset and polarization matter more than total γδ count.** Several corpus papers dissect this with single‑cell data, e.g. functional heterogeneity of γδ T cells in colorectal cancer (PMID 41953027) and CD69⁺ Vδ1 cells as the anti‑tumor subpopulation in HCC (PMID, "CD69+ Vδ1γδ T cells are anti‑tumor…").

## What controls the antitumor ↔ protumor fork
Determinants repeatedly implicated:
- **Subset:** Vδ1 and IFN‑γ⁺ Vγ9Vδ2 trend antitumor; γδ17 (often a distinct Vγ usage) trends protumor.
- **Cytokine milieu at priming:** IL‑12/IL‑18/IFN‑γ favor cytotoxic γδ1; **TGF‑β + IL‑1β + IL‑23 + IL‑6** favor γδ17.
- **Microbiome and chronic inflammation** (especially in gut/liver) skew toward γδ17.
- **Antigen/metabolic context:** butyrophilin dysregulation, lactate, hypoxia.

## Implications for therapy
1. **Engineer/expand away from IL‑17.** Manufacturing cytokine cocktails are deliberately chosen to enrich IFN‑γ⁺ cytotoxic cells and avoid γδ17 (favoring IL‑2/IL‑15/IL‑18 over IL‑23/IL‑1β/IL‑6).
2. **Prefer subsets less prone to γδ17** (a frequent argument for **Vδ1/DOT** platforms in some indications).
3. **Beware endogenous γδ17 when stimulating in vivo** (bisphosphonates, agonist antibodies could in principle expand the wrong cells in the wrong context).
4. **Stratify by tumor type** — the protumor risk is indication‑specific (notably colorectal, HCC, pancreatic, some breast).
