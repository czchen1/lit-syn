# Cancer‑type‑specific findings

γδ T cell biology and therapeutic prospects vary by tumor type. Below, the corpus is summarized by indication, with the dominant theme and representative entries (see `index.tsv` for full lists; tag counts in parentheses).

## Hematologic malignancies — the clearest wins

### Leukemia (203 tagged)
The most heavily represented indication.
- **AML:** Vδ1/DOT cells kill AML via **CD155/PVR–DNAM‑1** (PMID 38437507) and **KIR‑defined** effector programs (PMID 42044172); non‑viral "super Vδ2" CAR‑γδ for r/r AML (PMID 42284763); innate‑cell platforms for **ATLL** (PMID, "Development of Innate‑Immune‑Cell‑Based Immunotherapy for Adult T‑Cell Leukemia‑Lymphoma").
- **ALL/CLL:** γδ recognition of leukemic blasts; reconstituting γδ cells after αβ‑depleted haplo‑HSCT correlate with relapse protection.
- γδ cells are attractive here because of abundant stress‑ligand display and the allogeneic/transplant context.

### Lymphoma (76)
- **Allogeneic anti‑CD20 CAR‑Vδ1 (ADI‑001)** — preclinical PMID 35136603, clinical responses in r/r B‑NHL.
- **Haploidentical γδ cells** → complete remissions in chemorefractory B‑NHL.
- **CD19 AbTCR / γδ‑TCR‑T** in r/r DLBCL; mosunetuzumab co‑opting Vγ9Vδ2 (PMID "Two Are Better than One").

### Multiple myeloma (29)
- **CD1d×Vδ2 bispecific engagers** (LAVA platform) and Vγ9Vδ2 ADCC with anti‑CD38/anti‑SLAMF7; bisphosphonate sensitization. Caveat: myeloma can also harbor suppressive γδ.

## Solid tumors — the hard problem

### Breast (65)
The canonical **protumor** setting: IL‑17⁺ γδ T17 → neutrophil/MDSC‑driven metastasis (Coffelt 2015). Reviews stress the **dual role** (PMID 41161891). Therapeutic interest centers on Vδ1/cytotoxic skewing and HER2 ADCC/engagers.

### Colorectal (63)
Mixed/contradictory prognosis; strong **functional heterogeneity** by single‑cell analysis (PMID 41953027); CXCR3‑dependent **Delta One T** recruitment improves efficacy (PMID 42208977). γδ17 implicated in protumor inflammation.

### Lung / NSCLC (48)
Autologous zoledronate‑activated γδ therapy trials (PMID, NSCLC experience); systemic γδ immune‑parameter studies; ADCC combinations.

### Glioma / CNS (42)
A very active recent area: **intracranial MGMT‑modified γδ + temozolomide** for high‑grade glioma; **B7‑H3 CAR‑γδ intrathecal** for leptomeningeal metastasis; **γδ‑TCR‑T + oncolytic virus** for diffuse midline glioma (PMID 40076788); autologous Vδ2⁺ γδ‑TIL responses to glioblastoma show inter‑patient heterogeneity (PMID 41407399).

### Melanoma (44), HCC (37), Pancreatic (36), Prostate (31), Ovarian (29), Renal (24)
- **HCC:** liver is γδ‑rich; **CD69⁺ Vδ1** are the anti‑tumor subpopulation (PMID, "CD69+ Vδ1γδ T cells are anti‑tumor…"); intrasinusoidal γδ cytotoxicity is phosphoantigen‑dependent (PMID 36585162); γδ17 can be protumor.
- **Prostate:** historical Dieli 2007 zoledronate trial; γδ‑enriched CAR‑T for bone‑metastatic CRPC (PMID 37134157); review PMID 41110898.
- **Ovarian:** anti‑PD‑1‑armored γδ cells (PMID, armored γδ ovarian).
- **Pancreatic / melanoma / renal:** stress‑ligand recognition and ADCC/engager combinations; pancreatic notably immunosuppressive.

### Neuroblastoma (23)
- **GD2‑CAR Vγ9Vδ2** (Capsomidis 2018, Caforio 2021) and **antibody‑γ/δ‑TCR targeting GPC2** at low antigen density (PMID 41027430).

### Mesothelioma
- Three distinct γδ cytotoxic mechanisms dissected (PMID 37006249); anti‑PD‑1 combination (PMID 38077396).

## Cross‑cutting reading
- **Hematologic/transplant‑adjacent indications** are where γδ therapy has produced the clearest clinical responses to date.
- **Solid tumors** are gated by trafficking, the suppressive/acidic TME, and the protumor γδ17 risk — driving the Vδ1, armoring, and combination strategies.
- For almost every indication, **subset and polarization** (cytotoxic Vδ1/IFN‑γ⁺ vs γδ17) predict whether γδ presence is good or bad better than total γδ count.
