# Per‑paper capsule extractions

Capsule summaries of landmark and representative papers in the corpus. Each entry distills the central design/finding, not the full methods. `[status]` indicates whether a local PDF exists (`downloaded`) or the paper is catalogued only (`not_available_oa` / `no_pmcid`). See `index.tsv` for paths/IDs.

## Recognition / butyrophilin biology

- **Structures of butyrophilin multimers reveal a plier‑like mechanism for Vγ9Vδ2 TCR activation** — *2025* (PMID 40505658) `[no_pmcid]`. Structural model of how BTN3A1/BTN2A1 multimers engage the Vγ9Vδ2 TCR upon phosphoantigen sensing; mechanistic capstone of the two‑butyrophilin recognition model.
- **BTN2A1: A Novel Target to Boost Tumor Killing Capacity of Human γδ T Cells** — *2024* (PMID 39475356) `[no_pmcid]`. Frames BTN2A1 surface level as a tunable determinant of γδ cytotoxicity and a therapeutic/biomarker target.
- **EBV BRRF1 induces BTN2A1 in nasopharyngeal carcinoma** — *2024* (PMID 39769218) `[not_available_oa]`. Viral oncogene modulates the butyrophilin recognition axis, linking tumor virology to γδ recognition.

## Effector mechanism

- **Three distinct mechanisms underlying human γδ T cell‑mediated cytotoxicity against malignant pleural mesothelioma** — *2023* (PMID 37006249) `[downloaded]`. Dissects parallel killing routes (granule, death‑receptor, and pyroptosis‑associated) against MPM, underscoring mechanistic redundancy.
- **KIRs define a potent effector program in human γδ T cells** — *2026* (PMID 42044172) `[no_pmcid]`. Multi‑omic (flow/ATAC/RNA‑seq) identification of a KIR‑associated high‑cytotoxicity γδ program relevant to selecting potent effectors.
- **CD155/PVR determines AML targeting by Delta One T cells** — *2024, Blood* (PMID 38437507) `[not_available_oa]`. Identifies the DNAM‑1 ligand CD155 as the molecular determinant of Vδ1/DOT‑cell killing of AML blasts.

## Dual role / protumor

- **IL‑17‑producing γδ T cells in the TME promote radioresistance in mice** — *2025* (PMID 41055972) `[downloaded]`. Causally links γδ17/IL‑17 to radioresistance — a concrete liability for radiation + immunotherapy combinations.
- **Functional heterogeneity of γδ T cells in colorectal cancer** — *2026* (PMID 41953027) `[downloaded]`. Single‑cell dissection showing co‑existing antitumor and protumor γδ states within CRC.
- **Gamma Delta T Cells in the Tumour Microenvironment: A Double‑Edged Sword** — *2026* (PMID 41086872) `[no_pmcid]`. Review crystallizing the antitumor↔protumor duality framing used throughout this synthesis.

## Subsets / Vδ1 platform

- **Vδ1 T cells exhibit high lactic‑acid resistance and antitumor activity in solid tumors** — *2026* (PMID 42106736) `[no_pmcid]`. Scalable Vδ1 expansion (humanized Vδ1‑TCR antibody + cytokines) and evidence of TME‑acidosis resistance; tests unmodified and CAR‑Vδ1 cells.
- **CXCR3 signaling promotes Delta One T cell recruitment and antitumor efficacy in CRC** — *2026, JITC* (PMID 42208977) `[downloaded]`. Shows trafficking (CXCR3) is rate‑limiting for adoptive Vδ1/DOT efficacy in solid tumor.
- **Dual modulation of cytotoxic and checkpoint receptors tunes adoptive Delta One T cell therapy against CRC** — *2025, Nature Cancer* (PMID 40240620) `[downloaded]`. Engineering DOT cells by simultaneously boosting activating and dampening inhibitory receptors to improve solid‑tumor efficacy.

## Expansion / adoptive

- **Enhancing adoptive cancer immunotherapy with Vγ2Vδ2 T cells through pulse zoledronate stimulation** — *2017* (PMID 28239463) `[downloaded]`. Optimized pulsed‑zoledronate protocol to expand functional Vγ9Vδ2 cells while limiting exhaustion.
- **In‑vivo manipulation of Vγ9Vδ2 T cells with zoledronate and low‑dose IL‑2** — *2010* (PMID 20491785) `[downloaded]`. Prototype of in‑vivo (vs ex‑vivo) Vγ9Vδ2 activation strategy.
- **A clinical‑grade automated platform for the manufacturing of CAR‑γδ T cells** — *2026* (PMID 42253960) `[downloaded]`. Closed/automated GMP manufacturing tailored to CAR‑γδ products — a translational enabler.

## Engineering

- **Allogeneic CD20‑targeted γδ T cells exhibit innate and adaptive antitumor activities** — *2022* (PMID 35136603) `[not_available_oa]`. Preclinical foundation for **ADI‑001** (allogeneic anti‑CD20 CAR‑Vδ1): premanufactured healthy‑donor product combining CAR and innate killing.
- **Antibody‑gamma/delta TCR (AbTCR) targeting GPC2 regress neuroblastoma with low antigen density** — *2025, Cell Rep Med* (PMID 41027430) `[downloaded]`. AbTCR design leverages γδ‑TCR signaling sensitivity to hit low‑density GPC2 where conventional CARs fail.
- **γδ‑Enriched CAR‑T cell therapy for bone‑metastatic castrate‑resistant prostate cancer** — *2023, Sci Adv* (PMID 37134157) `[downloaded]`. PSCA‑targeted γδ CAR‑T combined with zoledronate in a bone mCRPC model — combination engineering + sensitization.
- **Development of super Vδ2 T cells for r/r AML via non‑viral knock‑in** — *2026* (PMID 42284763) `[no_pmcid]`. Non‑viral (transposon/CRISPR) CAR‑γδ manufacturing for AML.
- **CAR‑engineering of innate and innate‑like immune cells** — *2026* (PMID 42082268) `[downloaded]`. Review situating CAR‑γδ within the broader innate‑cell engineering landscape (vs CAR‑NK, CAR‑NKT, CAR‑macrophage).

## Combinations / clinical

- **Unusual Partners: γδ‑TCR‑based T cell therapy + oncolytic virus for diffuse midline glioma** — *2025* (PMID 40076788) `[downloaded]`. Combines γδ‑TCR‑T with oncolytic virotherapy to overcome the immunosuppressive CNS TME.
- **Autologous profiling reveals inter‑patient heterogeneity in Vδ2⁺ γδ‑TIL responses to glioblastoma** — *2025* (PMID 41407399) `[downloaded]`. Patient‑level variability in tumor‑infiltrating Vδ2 responses, motivating stratification.
- **Advances in IL‑15‑based cancer immunotherapy and divergent effects of IL‑2 vs IL‑15** — *2026* (PMID 42039157) `[downloaded]`. Synthesizes the IL‑2‑vs‑IL‑15 trade‑off central to γδ persistence and manufacturing.
