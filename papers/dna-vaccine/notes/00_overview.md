# DNA vaccines for glioblastoma: overview

## What this collection covers

21 papers in which a **nucleic-acid construct is given as a vaccine to raise anti-glioma immunity**, spanning **1999/2003 → 2026**. The field is small and largely preclinical (GL261, 9L, C6, U251, intracranial models), with a single first-in-human DNA-vaccine trial reported in 2026.

## Eras and inflection points

- **2003 — first dedicated glioma DNA vaccine (O et al., *Cancer Gene Therapy*; PMID 12944987)**: a plasmid encoding human TRP-2 induces CD8⁺ T-cell-mediated, partial protection against TRP-2⁺ GL261 mouse glioblastoma (s.c., i.v., and intracerebral challenge). This established TRP-2/gp100 melanoma-associated antigens as usable glioma DNA-vaccine targets because GL261 aberrantly expresses melanin-biosynthesis proteins.
- **2004–2005 — anti-angiogenic DNA vaccines (Feng et al.; PMIDs 15587406, 15890095, 15797625, 15650458)**: a Huazhong/Wuhan group immunized against the tumor-endothelium self-antigen VEGFR2 (flk-1) using **orally delivered attenuated *Salmonella typhimurium* SL7207** carrying the plasmid, alone or combined with an IL-12 plasmid, slowing intracranial G261 growth, reducing microvessel density, and prolonging survival.
- **2005–2008 — antigen and adjuvant diversification**: Sindbis **DNA-launched** replicon encoding gp100 + IL-18 (Yamanaka 2005; PMID 15869409); a DC-survival "adjuvant gene" Bcl-xl co-delivered with TRP2-hsp70 DNA to prolong antigen presentation (Kim 2005; PMID 16052205); and a SOX6 cancer-testis-antigen DNA vaccine giving protective and therapeutic immunity without autoimmunity (Ueda 2008; PMID 18224680).
- **2010–2011 — delivery vectors and epitope optimization**: live attenuated *Pseudomonas* type-III-secretion delivery of TRP-2/gp100/MUC18 antigens from an inducible plasmid (Derouazi 2010; PMID 21327126); EphA2 peptide + LIGHT plasmid combination eliciting CTL against glioma U251 (Chen 2011; PMID 22032907).
- **2013 — honest negative/limited result (Ginzkey et al., *Acta Neurochir*; PMID 23132370)**: intramuscular DNA vaccination against a model antigen (lacZ) in syngeneic 9L rat gliosarcoma reduced tumor size but achieved only **incomplete control**, foreshadowing the need for combination regimens.
- **2021–2023 — the pTOP platform and rational combinations (Préat/Vandermeulen group, UCLouvain)**: pTOP encodes an engineered VSV-G that carries foreign T-cell tumor epitopes, exploiting the innate immunogenicity of the viral glycoprotein (Lopes 2021; PMID 33795383). It is then combined with **dual immune-checkpoint blockade** in orthotopic GL261 (Bausart 2022; PMID 35631612) and with **immunogenic-cell-death chemotherapy** (mitoxantrone nanoparticles; Bausart 2023; PMID 37105343).
- **2022 — multi-antigen platform vaccines**: ITI-1001 encodes three HCMV antigens (pp65, gB, IE-1) on the **UNITE/LAMP1 lysosomal-targeting** backbone to boost MHC-I and -II presentation (Adhikari 2022; PMID 35651802), leveraging HCMV-protein expression in GBM.
- **2024 — antibody-DNA-vaccine + checkpoint (Pearson et al.; PMID 38954031)**: SCIB1, a TRP-2/gp100 **ImmunoBody®** DNA vaccine, combined with anti-PD-1 prolongs survival in intracranial TRP-2/gp100⁺ tumors and increases CD4/CD8 infiltration.
- **2026 — first-in-human DNA vaccine for GBM (Garfinkle et al., *Nature Cancer*; PMID 42120910, with a *Nature* news feature PMID 42151541)**: the **GT-20 phase 1** of **GNOS-PV01**, a DNA-based personalized multivalent **neoantigen** vaccine (up to 40 neoantigens/patient) + **IL-12 plasmid (INO-9012)** adjuvant delivered by **CELLECTRA electroporation** as adjuvant therapy after resection + radiation in **MGMT-unmethylated** GBM; 9 patients, safe, immunogenic (T-cell activation correlated with OS), median OS 16.3 mo and 33% 24-month survival incl. one >4-year survivor (see `03`).

## Recurring scientific themes

- **Combination is the rule, monotherapy is weak.** Multiple groups independently report that a glioma DNA vaccine alone gives partial/incomplete control (Ginzkey 2013; O 2003) and pair it with checkpoint blockade (Bausart 2022, Pearson 2024), cytokines/IL-12 (Feng 2004/2005), immunogenic-cell-death chemo (Bausart 2023), or DC-survival genes (Kim 2005).
- **Antigen-spreading vs. self-tolerance.** Self/differentiation antigens (TRP-2, VEGFR2) require breaking tolerance, motivating xenogeneic antigens (human TRP-2/gp100 in mice), hsp70 fusions, and viral-glycoprotein carriers to add danger signals.
- **Vector innate immunity as built-in adjuvant.** The recurring use of live bacteria (*Salmonella*, *Pseudomonas*), alphavirus replicons, and VSV-G reflects a deliberate strategy to supply innate co-stimulation that naked DNA lacks.
- **Translation lag.** Despite ~20 years of preclinical work, the first human GBM DNA-vaccine trial (GNOS-PV01) appears only in 2026 and uses personalized neoantigens + electroporation + IL-12 plasmid adjuvant rather than the single-shared-antigen designs that dominate the preclinical literature.

## How to read the notes

`01`–`03` are topic syntheses drawing on the 8 open-access full texts plus the abstracts of the 13 paywalled papers (clearly flagged where only the abstract was available). `04` documents the scope boundary. Per-paper detail is folded into the topic notes rather than a separate extractions file, given the small corpus.
